# Documentation instructions

Keep owner decisions, explicit design proposals, observed implementation facts and unverified hypotheses distinct. Do not turn a proposed command, risk value, preset appearance or test into a verified claim. Use current official references for library/provider facts and keep implementation evidence separate.

Update contracts before or with consequential implementation changes. Preserve initial paper-only scope and historical approvals/results. Maintain relative links, readable task-specific documents and scoped plans rather than one enormous root instruction file. New information never silently erases prior experiment history.

In v2, keep permission authority in AUTONOMY_MATRIX, risk values and the rule's definitions in RISK_POLICY and trading judgment in TRADING. Since ExecPlan 0060 the app has no runtime agents: their contracts (AGENT_TOOL_CONTRACTS, RUNTIME_AGENTS, RESEARCH_WORKFLOWS, EVALUATION, ADAPTATION) are in [archive/](archive/README.md), history only. Change every affected contract when authority changes; do not append contradictory “override” documents. Maintain the requirement-to-test checklist and migration/change inventory.
