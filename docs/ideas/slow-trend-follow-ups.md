# The slow trend twin's follow-ups

**PROPOSED, 2026-11-07.** Ideas the build of [ExecPlan 0057](../archive/plans/0057-a-slow-trend-twin.md) left out, in the project thread "Add a slow trend twin". Nothing in this note is decided, and none of it restarts the trader's test: each one is a twin or an offline check. Every number is `CALCULATED` on the thread's offline check of 2022-01-01 to 2026-10-31 ([0057 §4.7](../archive/plans/0057-a-slow-trend-twin.md#47-the-offline-check-calculated-backtest_modeled_not_filled)), labeled `BACKTEST_MODELED_NOT_FILLED`; the scripts are in the project's shared folder `slow-trend/`.

| Idea | What it would change | What the offline check says | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Partial resizes | The twin trades only the difference between what it holds and its target, instead of closing the whole position and opening the target | 19,974 against 19,009 for the whole-position resize: 736 USDT less in fees over 4.8 years (`rebal2.py`) | A new money path for the twins: a position that grows or shrinks, with its fills, wallet check, valuations and pages; a new twin set |
| The long-or-flat arm | The same rule, flat instead of short when the trend points down | 20,721 against 20,037 for long or short, a worst fall of 21% against 29%; both `UNCLEAR` against holding | A second twin, or a reading of the stored `DAY_SIGNAL` rows: each stores the signal, so the arm's path is recomputable without a twin |
| A wider buffer | Resize only when the target moves by more than half the wallet, not a quarter | 20,589 against 19,050 with whole-position resizes (`rebal.py`) | Chosen after seeing the result, so it would be tuning: only as a new rule with its own pre-registered measure, if the twin's own record ever argues for it |
| The rule on the main account | The owner's account follows the rule instead of, or beside, the trader | Not measured beyond the twin's offline check | The owner's choice; the twin's paired measure against holding (`BEATS_HOLD`) first |
| The momentum twin's waiting flip | When `MOMENTUM_1W`'s flip exit cannot fill (a book too thin for it, or a fill beyond the liquidation price without a fresh mark), the protection retries it every second; if it waits past midnight and the next day's reading keeps the side, it can still fill and leave the twin flat against that reading.
