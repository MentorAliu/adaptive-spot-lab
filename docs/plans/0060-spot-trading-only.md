## 1. Goal

After one update:
1. The main account trades Bitcoin **spot** on paper: it holds USDT and BTC and buys and sells BTCUSDT at the prices of Binance's public order book. Nothing is borrowed, nothing is sold short, and no perpetual, margin, leverage, funding or liquidation is left anywhere in the app.
2. It buys and sells by `SPOT_RULE_V1` (§4.1), code: once a UTC day it reads Bitcoin's trend over the last 1, 3 and 12 months; when the trend is up it holds a slice of the money in Bitcoin sized to its volatility, and when the trend is not up it holds cash. It trades only the difference to its target, and only when the difference is large enough to be worth the fees.
3. The account's protections stay: new buying stops after a 2% loss in a UTC day or an 8% fall from the run's high, the drawdown stop ends the run and sells, and the owner's Pause, Resume, Stop, Stop-and-sell, Start and top-ups work as today.
4. The app **runs its own backtest** of the same rule on Bitcoin's whole spot history from Binance's public data, at the install and again after each finished day, and shows it beside holding Bitcoin (§4.6).
5. Four pages: Cockpit, Trades (the buys, the sells and the daily readings), Backtest and Settings.
6. Everything that served the futures account goes, with its tests and backtests: the perpetual's feed, the futures account and its policies, the AI trader and its reviewers, coach, veto, scores, result checks, twins, league, replay and AI backtests; and with them what the futures app had already switched off (the pre-futures spot engine, the research, the coin scan, the news sources). Their records stay in the database, read-only (§4.7).

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The rule `SPOT_RULE_V1` (code)

`MAIN_RULE_V1`'s signal, volatility and size, on spot candles, trading the difference:

| Part | Rule | From |
|---|---|---|
| When | Once a UTC day, at the engine's first step at or after 00:05 UTC once the previous day's final daily candle is stored; a day not readable yet is tried again every 15 minutes; a day whose reading filled or needed no change is spent | 0059 §4.1 |
| The direction | `s` = the mean of the signs of the last complete day's close against the closes 30, 91 and 365 days before it (each the nearest stored close within 7 days), then `max(s, 0)`: never short | TR-01, TR-03; 0057's T2 |
| The volatility | `σ` = the annualized (365 days) exponentially weighted standard deviation of daily log returns, centre of mass 60 days, at least 300 of the last 366 days stored | TR-01, TR-10 |
| The target | `f = max(s, 0) · min(1, 0.05 / σ)` of equity, quantized toward zero to 0.001, 0 below 0.01 | §4.2 |
| Acting | `held` = the BTC held, valued at the best bid, over equity. When `f = 0` and BTC is held: **sell it all**. Otherwise, when `|f − held| > 0.03`: **buy or sell the difference** `(f − held) · equity` at the book; else nothing | spot: no whole resize, no residual holding |
| Exits | Only the rule's sells, the owner's Stop-and-sell, and a stopping run (the drawdown stop or the owner's plain Stop), which sells all at once | 0059's review |
| Costs | `SPOT_COST_V1` (§4.3) | |

Two changes from `MAIN_RULE_V1`, both for spot and both fixed here before the build: a resize trades only the difference (spot has no position to close, and it halves the fees: §4.2), and a zero target sells everything (0059 kept a holding of up to 3% of equity when the signal fell to zero; "never short" now means cash when the trend is not up).

Each reading is stored once a day with its day, close, anchors, `s`, `σ`, `f`, `held`, the action, the outcome (`FILLED`, `NO_CHANGE`, `NOT_READABLE`, `REFUSED` with its codes) and its fill, so every reading can be recomputed from the stored candles; the backtest of §4.6 computes the same reading for the same day with the same function.

### 4.2 The size, and what history shows

The size keeps 0059's rule, written before its sizing run: the largest of 40, 30, 20, 15, 10 and 5% a year whose worst fall from the high, counted on each day's intraday low, stays at or below 4%, half the 8% drawdown stop. It picks **5%**, as it did for the perpetual: spot changes the fees and the funding, not how far Bitcoin falls. Raising the 8% stop is not proposed ([TRADING](../TRADING.md#what-not-to-propose)).

`CALCULATED` by the thread on 2026-11-12 (`BACKTEST_MODELED_NOT_FILLED`: fills at the daily close, a 0.10% fee and 1 bp of slippage on each trade, the latches applied at the close; the script and its output are in the project's shared folder, `spot/spot_numbers.py` and `spot_numbers.json`). The data is the BTCUSDT perpetual's daily candles from Binance's public archive (the 0059 thread's files, 2020-01-01 to 2026-10-31, one day missing), standing in for spot's because the cloud cannot reach Binance; spot and perpetual closes differ by the basis, usually under 0.1%. The app's own backtest on spot candles (§4.6) replaces these numbers at the install. From 10,000 USDT:

| 2022-01-01 | 11,433 | 2.8% | 3.4% | 16,758 | 68.0% |
| 2023-01-01 | 11,433 | 3.6% | 3.4% | 46,825 | 54.2% |

From 2021: 152 trades (72 buys, 80 sells, about two a month), 102 of fees, Bitcoin held on 53% of days at 3.7% of the money on average and 16.9% at most, a worst day of −1.2%, volatility 2.9% a year (Sharpe 1.0); by year 2021 +3.8%, 2022 0.0% (in cash nearly all year), 2023 +5.6%, 2024 +7.3%, 2025 +0.7%, 2026 to October +0.1%. At 5 bp of slippage 11,824; at the 0.075% fee with Binance's BNB discount 11,889. The perpetual's version with whole resizes ended at 11,245 over the same span (0059 §4.2): spot pays no funding (392 over the span) and difference trades halve the fees.

Read plainly: the rule makes a small, steady amount and avoids Bitcoin's big falls; holding Bitcoin made more in the rises and lost half or more in the falls. Inside the 8% stop the rule cannot hold more: at a 10% target it would have ended at 13,626 with a worst fall of 6.6%, and at 20% the 8% stop would have ended its run on 2024-08-11 (not proposed). Nothing guarantees a profit; five years of one market cannot separate the rule from luck (TRADING principle 10).

### 4.3 `SPOT_POLICY_V1` and `SPOT_COST_V1`

Two code-owned documents (`app.spot.definitions`) seeded by migration `0041` with their hashes, as 0039's and 0059's; the worker starts only when the stored rows equal the code, and each run records both hashes and the rule's as `SYSTEM`.

| `SPOT_POLICY_V1` | Value |
|---|---|
| Instrument | BTCUSDT spot; holdings USDT and BTC; long or cash |
| Who decides | `SPOT_RULE_V1`, code, as `SYSTEM`; no model decides anything |
| Actions | `RULE_BUY`, `RULE_SELL` (the rule's daily trade), `STOP_SELL` (a stopping run or the owner's Stop-and-sell sells all) |
| Size | The rule's target; never more than the free USDT after the fee and slippage, never more BTC than held; at most 1x by construction (no borrowing exists) |
| Latches | Daily loss 2% of the UTC day's reference NAV; drawdown 8% from the run's high-water NAV (unchanged). Effect: no buy; sells continue; the drawdown stop makes the run stopping, which sells all at once and stops once only USDT is held |
| Runs | `RUNNING`, `PAUSED` (a cause open), `STOPPING`, `STOPPED`, as M11's; the first run starts by itself, any later one is the owner's Start |
| Data | A buy or sell needs a book at most 2 s old with the instrument usable and its clock verified; the valuation needs a best bid at most 5 s old (`VALUATION_BID_V1`, M3's: cash plus BTC at the best bid, floored to 8 places); a stale or missing price refuses the trade with its code and invalidates the valuation, nothing is guessed |
| Valuation | Every 60 s while BTC is held, at each fill, each day's reference and each new high; hourly otherwise |

| `SPOT_COST_V1` | Value |
|---|---|
| Fee | 0.10% of each fill's notional, the rate Binance states for a regular user (`OBSERVED`, §7), charged in USDT. Binance charges it in the asset received (BTC on a buy); charging the same value in USDT keeps BTC in whole lot steps and changes the result by less than a cent a trade |
| Slippage | 1 bp of the notional against the account, on top of walking the stored book |
| Fill | A market order walks the stored book's levels (asks for a buy, bids for a sell) and is refused when they cannot fill it (`DEPTH_SHORT`); quantity in `LOT_SIZE`/`MARKET_LOT_SIZE` steps, refused below the minimum quantity or notional (`NOTIONAL` with `applyMinToMarket`), read from the instrument at the trade |

### 4.4 The money path

- **Postings** (`app.spot.ledger`, the only writer): each fill posts one journal transaction whose source is the fill, M3's per-asset double entry: a buy moves `OWNER_CASH` −gross and `CLEARING` +gross in USDT, `CLEARING` −qty and `OWNER_ASSET` +qty in BTC; a sell the reverse; the fee `OWNER_CASH` −fee and `EXPENSE` +fee in USDT. The account's cash, `account_holdings`' BTC and the account version change in the same transaction; the database refuses negative cash or BTC.
- **The step** (`app.spot.engine`), one fenced transaction a second, in this order: the owner's requests; the first run by itself; a stopping run's sell (or the owner's Stop-and-sell); the valuation, the day's reference and the latches; the run's state; the rule's daily reading and its trade. The fencing, the lease, the requests, the runs, the latches and the readings follow `app.futures`, whose code they replace.
- **Reconciliation**: the cash and the BTC in `account_state` and `account_holdings` equal the journal's `OWNER_CASH` and `OWNER_ASSET` sums and the fills', no reservation is open, every fill has its journal transaction; a mismatch opens `INTEGRITY`, as today.
- **Top-ups** stay NAV-neutral (rule 12): issued at a valid valuation, as M1 made them.

### 4.5 The market data (`app.spot_market`)

The market worker runs the spot feed instead of the perpetual's, from Binance's market-data-only hosts M2 used ([EX-01](../REFERENCES.md#ex-01)), with M2's client checks (fixed hosts, checked public address, TLS, no credentials, capped answers, the weight guard):
- one combined stream from `data-stream.binance.vision`: `btcusdt@bookTicker`, `btcusdt@depth10@100ms` and `btcusdt@kline_1m`; the newest book of each second is stored (`spot_quotes`, kept 2 days), each 1-minute candle as it changes and final once closed (`spot_bars`, kept 30 days for the chart);
- REST from `data-api.binance.vision`: the instrument (`exchangeInfo`) at the start and every 30 minutes, the clock (`time`) every minute, missing 1-minute candles after a gap, and **every final daily candle since 2017-08-17** (`klines`, `interval=1d`, 1,000 a request: four requests the first time, then one after each 00:00 UTC) into `spot_daily_bars`, kept for good;
- the feed's state (`CONNECTED` only while the stream delivers, the clock is verified and the instrument usable) and its events, as the perpetual's feed.

### 4.6 The backtest in the app (`SPOT_BACKTEST_V1`)

The execution worker computes the rule's backtest from `spot_daily_bars` after the install and again once a new final day is stored, outside the money step, and stores one row per computation (`spot_backtests`: the rule's and the cost's hashes, the data's first and last day and a hash of the candles, the results). Each computation covers three windows, from 10,000 USDT: **since 2019-01-01** (the first full year after the rule's 365-day warm-up), **since 2023-01-01** and **the last 12 months**. Each holds the rule and holding Bitcoin side by side: the final value, the return a year, the worst fall (intraday, from each day's low), the fees, the buys and sells, the share of days in Bitcoin, a row per calendar year and the daily values for the chart.

It uses the live rule's own reading function (§4.1) and `SPOT_COST_V1`'s fee and slippage, fills at the daily close (labeled `BACKTEST_MODELED_NOT_FILLED` on the page), and applies the latches as the live account would: a day whose low crosses the 2% daily loss buys nothing that day; a fall to 8% from the run's high sells everything at the close and holds cash for the rest of the window, shown as "the run would have stopped here". It replaces 0051's futures backtest and the AI backtests; it needs no model and costs a few milliseconds.

### 4.10 The measure, pre-registered

- **M1, the money:** the main account's daily log return under `SPOT_POLICY_V1` against cash, stationary bootstrap (mean block 3 days, 10,000 resamples), 95%: `MAKES_MONEY`, `LOSES_MONEY` or `UNCLEAR`; too few under 90 days; read by the weekly review from 90 days, then quarterly. Expected `UNCLEAR` for years (`CALCULATED`: 3% a year at a volatility near 3% needs about four to eight years to reach t = 2, TR-35).
- **M2, the limit:** the run's worst fall from its high-water NAV, read weekly; above 4% is a design breach the weekly review reports with a card; the 8% stop acts in code whatever it reads.
- **M3, the implementation:** each live reading equals the backtest's reading for the same day on the same stored candles (a test, and the weekly review on the PC's rows); each fill's quantity within one lot step of `|f − held| · equity / price`.
- Descriptive, beside: the account against holding Bitcoin from the run's start, paired by day.

Trial ledger: the slow-rule family's sixth variant (0057's T1 and T2, 0059's three offline checks and its main-account rule, counted in 0059 §4.6 as five); the size is set by the limit, not by a result; the two changes of §4.1 are design choices for spot, made before this plan's numbers and kept whatever they show. Nothing changes from a verdict without an update the owner installs.

### 4.11 The trading review ([TRADING.md](../TRADING.md#how-to-judge-a-trading-change)'s eight questions)

1. **Rules:** paper only, spot, long or cash, code decides every size and trade; no limit moves; the latches and the owner's controls stay. Rule 1 changes back to spot only, on the owner's word (§5).
2. **Costs:** about two trades a month of 2 to 17% of the money each; 0.11% a trade against moves the rule holds for weeks; fees 1% of the money over six years (`CALCULATED`, §4.2).
3. **Horizon:** 1- to 12-month signals for holds of weeks (TR-01, TR-03, principle 4).
4. **The model's place:** none; no model trades, reads or vetoes.
5. **The yardstick:** M1 against cash, M2 the limit, M3 the implementation; holding Bitcoin descriptively; "too few" until 90 days.
6. **Pre-registration:** §4.10, written before the first reading.
7. **The trial ledger:** six in the family (§4.10).
8. **The clock and the cost:** no model calls at all from the install (the agents' allowance is no longer used); 0059's M1 and M2 end with the futures run and are read descriptively over its days; the twins' readings end with the twins.

## 11. Failure and rollback

- The daily candles are late or missing: the day is `NOT_READABLE` and tried again every 15 minutes; the holding stays as it is; the Cockpit says so. The backtest keeps its last computation.
- The book is stale or thin at the reading: the trade is refused with its code and tried again at the next 15 minutes; the day is not spent.
- The futures account is not flat at the install: the update stops before changing anything (§4.7).
- The rule loses: M1 `LOSES_MONEY` or M2's breach goes to the owner on a card; nothing changes by itself.
- **Rollback:** the update's backup restores the futures database whole; going back in code is a later update (the futures code stays in the git history). Migration `0041`'s downgrade refuses once a spot fill exists: a rollback after that goes forward.
