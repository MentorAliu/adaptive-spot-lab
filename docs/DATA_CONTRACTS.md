# Data contracts and provenance

The records the spot app keeps ([ExecPlan 0060](plans/0060-spot-trading-only.md), M12; [ExecPlan 0061](plans/0061-clean-up-after-the-spot-switch.md)). The records of M2 to M11 (the M2 market tables, M3's broker, the experiments, the agents, the research, the coins, the futures) and their sections are in the [archived copy](archive/contracts/DATA_CONTRACTS.md); their rows are kept read-only in the database's `archive` schema (below).

## Common rules

- **Exact values.** Amounts are canonical decimal strings over HTTP/JSON and `Decimal`/`NUMERIC` in the engine, never binary floating point: prices, quantities and amounts `NUMERIC(38,8)`, units and NAV `NUMERIC(72,36)`, the rule's indicators unconstrained `NUMERIC`. Rounding follows the number policy of [PAPER_EXECUTION](PAPER_EXECUTION.md). Database sequences and cursors cross HTTP as decimal strings, so they keep their precision above JavaScript's safe integers.
- **Times.** UTC, with explicit precision: the provider's times as UTC milliseconds, the app's as timestamps with time zone. A time the app generated (a receipt, a valuation) is never presented as the provider's.
- **States and unknowns.** States are enums, not free text. A valuation is `VALID` or `INVALID` with its reason; the feed's staleness and a missing candle are derived at read time from the stored times. Over the API an unavailable value is `null` with its reason where it could be misread; zero means an actual zero.
- **Provenance.** Each fill copies the book levels it walked, the instrument it used and the cost definition's rate inline; each reading copies the close, the signal, the volatility and the target it computed, with the rule's id and hash; each valuation copies the bid it used and the account version it valued. A record is never rewritten to say what the app knew later.
- **Test data stays apart.** Fixtures and synthetic observations are written only to disposable `asl_m1_` databases with `ASL_TEST_ISOLATED=1`; normal collection uses Binance's public market data only.

## Market-data semantics

The market worker writes BTCUSDT's public spot data from Binance's market-data-only hosts ([MARKET_DATA](MARKET_DATA.md)). Every answer is parsed strictly: a payload that does not have the documented shape is refused, never guessed.
- A book row (`spot_quotes`) is the newest book of one second of receipt, the best bid and ask and ten levels a side; its quantities are displayed liquidity, and the account walks them as a model of a fill, not as a promise of one.
- A 1-minute candle (`spot_bars`) changes only until the provider marks it closed (or a REST answer returns it closed before the provider's clock); then it is final and never changes. Longer chart candles are calculated from the stored minutes and say when a minute is missing; a gap is shown, never filled.
- A daily candle (`spot_daily_bars`) is stored only once final and never replaced. The rule reads only daily candles that were final before its decision: a day's close cannot support a fill before that day ended.
- A candle whose event time lies more than two seconds ahead of the provider's clock as measured is dropped. Writes are idempotent by their keys (a book by its second, a candle by its open time), so a retry writes nothing twice.

## Historical and forward boundaries

The backtest (`SPOT_BACKTEST_V1`) runs the same rule code on the stored daily candles only, with a hash of the candles it used, and is shown as a modeled history, never mixed with the account's forward fills. A forward record is never backdated: PC downtime shows as late or missing readings and valuations, not as replayed ones.

## Schema evolution

Migrations are forward-only on the PC and preserve audit history; a correction is a new record, not an edit. Readiness accepts only the schema the code was built for (`app.db.ACCEPTED_REVISIONS`). The API's OpenAPI schema is generated from the implemented DTOs and the web's types from it; representative decimal, time and status fields are tested on both sides.

## The archive

Migration `0041_spot` moved every table of the earlier app (the futures, the agents, the research, M3's broker, the M2 market tables) with every row to the schema `archive`, readable by the migration role only, and recorded each move in `archive.moved_objects` (131 tables and 62 views on the owner's PC, `OBSERVED` on 2026-11-12). Nothing in the running app reads or writes it; the owner chose to keep it ([ExecPlan 0061 §13](plans/0061-clean-up-after-the-spot-switch.md#13-decision-log)), so a run's loss history stays.

These are the records in use.

### Migration `0041_spot`

- **The switch, in one transaction.** It refuses unless the futures account is flat (no open position, no pending funding payment, no active reservation, nothing reserved), with a message that names what is not, and changes nothing then. A live futures run ends as `SYSTEM` (`STOPPED`, `stop_reason` `NEW_POLICY`, a `FUTURES_RETIRED` run event with the three spot definitions' hashes, its open latches resolved), and pending futures owner requests are `REJECTED` (`FUTURES_RETIRED`).
- **The archive.** Every table of `public` but the kept ones moves to a schema `archive` with every row, with the sequences no kept table owns and the views of `agent_read`, `evaluation_read` and `research_read` (renamed `<schema>__<view>`); each move is recorded in `archive.moved_objects`. The schema is readable by the migration role only. Kept in `public`: `account_state`, `account_holdings`, `journal_transactions`, `journal_postings`, `funding_requests`, `execution_definitions`, `execution_incidents`, `execution_lease`, `outbox_events`, `audit_events`, `owner_decisions`, `system_metadata` and `alembic_version`.
- **The market tables** (written by `asl_market_worker`, read by `asl_execution` and `asl_api`): `spot_instruments` (each distinct `exchangeInfo` answer with its hash and the filters as exact NUMERIC), `spot_quotes` (one book a second: the best bid and ask and the ten levels a side; deleted after 2 days), `spot_bars` (1-minute candles, `STREAM` or `REST`, final once closed; deleted after 30 days), `spot_daily_bars` (every final daily candle, kept), `spot_feed_state` (one row) and `spot_feed_events`. A book row never changes and is deleted only after 2 days; a 1-minute candle changes only until it is final and is deleted only after 30 days; daily candles, instruments and feed events are append-only.
- **The account tables** (written by `asl_execution`; `asl_api` reads them and inserts `spot_owner_requests`, only a request's own columns: `id`, `kind`, `run_id`, `idempotency_key` and `request_hash`): `spot_runs` (the three definitions' ids and hashes and the code identity, `started_by` `SYSTEM` or `OWNER`, the state, the open causes among `OWNER`, `DAILY_LOSS`, `DRAWDOWN` and `INTEGRITY`, the start equity and NAV, the run's high-water NAV, the stop reason `OWNER` or `DRAWDOWN`; at most one run not stopped), `spot_owner_requests` (`PAUSE`, `RESUME`, `STOP`, `START`, by idempotency key and request hash, `PENDING` until the engine answers `APPLIED` or `REJECTED`), `spot_run_events`, `spot_fills` (the reason `RULE_BUY`, `RULE_SELL` or `STOP_SELL`, the quantity, the price, the walked average, the slippage, the gross, the fee in USDT and its rate, the levels walked, the instrument, the cash and BTC after, the account version), `spot_valuations` (`VALUATION_BID_V1`'s equity and NAV with the bid used and the account version valued, or `INVALID` with its reason; the purpose `START`, `FILL`, `PERIODIC`, `HOURLY`, `DAILY_REFERENCE`, `HIGH_WATER`, `LATCH`, `STOP` or `ACCOUNT_CHANGE`, the first step after a top-up), `spot_daily_references` (`TIMELY` or `LATE_CONSERVATIVE`), `spot_latches` (`DAILY_LOSS` or `DRAWDOWN`, at most one open per run and cause) and `spot_rule_readings` (the run, the UTC day, the rule and its hash, the result `LONG`, `CASH` or `WARMUP`, the close, the signal, the volatility, the target, the share held, the action `BUY`, `SELL`, `SELL_ALL` or `NO_CHANGE`, the outcome `NOT_READABLE`, `NO_CHANGE`, `FILLED` or `REFUSED` with its codes, the fill, the features). Fills, events, valuations, references and readings are append-only; a run changes only its state, causes, high-water NAV (never down) and stop, a stopped run never changes and a stopping one never runs again; a request changes only by its one answer, a latch only by its one resolution.
- **`spot_backtests`** (written by `asl_execution`, read by `asl_api`): one row per computation, `SPOT_BACKTEST_V1`, the rule's and the cost's hashes, the data's first and last day and a hash of the candles, the three windows' results; only its `series` (the daily values) may be cleared, once a newer row carries them.
- **The journal** gains `spot_fill_id` as a posting source: one journal transaction per fill, posted only by `asl_execution`; the retired sources take no new transaction.
- **Definitions:** `SPOT_RULE_V1`, `SPOT_POLICY_V1` and `SPOT_COST_V1` frozen in `execution_definitions` with their hashes (kinds `SPOT_RULE`, `SPOT_POLICY`, `SPOT_COST`).
- **Downgrade** to `0040` refuses once a spot fill or a spot journal transaction exists, or when the stored definitions differ ("fix forward or restore the backup"); otherwise it drops the spot tables, moves every archived object back and restores the checks.
