### Indicator warm-up sensitivity

OBSERVED in the code at `400de44`: `ema_series` in [indicators.py](https://github.com/MentorAliu/adaptive_spot_lab/blob/44b9a8a/services/engine/app/strategies/indicators.py) seeds each EMA with the simple average of the first complete period **of the run it is given**; Wilder ATR is seeded from the run's first true ranges. The engine evaluates the maximal contiguous run of finalized 1h bars, at most `run_window_max_bars` = 720 ([engine.py](https://github.com/MentorAliu/adaptive_spot_lab/blob/44b9a8a/services/engine/app/execution/engine.py)), and a missing or unresolved hour restarts the run. `TREND_REFERENCE_V1` signals once the run reaches `warmup_bars` = 100 ([definitions.py](../../services/engine/app/execution/definitions.py), [trend_reference_v1.py](https://github.com/MentorAliu/adaptive_spot_lab/blob/44b9a8a/services/engine/app/strategies/trend_reference_v1.py)). A research candidate may declare a warm-up as short as its longest feature period ([grammar.py](https://github.com/MentorAliu/adaptive_spot_lab/blob/44b9a8a/services/engine/app/research/grammar.py), `candidate_warmup_lookback`); the envelope allows lookbacks up to 200 and warm-ups up to 300 bars ([research definitions](https://github.com/MentorAliu/adaptive_spot_lab/blob/44b9a8a/services/engine/app/research/definitions.py)). The market worker backfills up to 720 prior 1h candles at start and about hourly afterwards ([worker.py](https://github.com/MentorAliu/adaptive_spot_lab/blob/44b9a8a/services/engine/app/market_data/worker.py) `backfill`, [MARKET_DATA](../MARKET_DATA.md)), and a backfilled final bar feeds indicators from its actual availability time. So a gap the provider can fill shortens the run only until the next backfill; bars evaluated meanwhile are `WARMUP_INCOMPLETE` while the run is below 100 bars. Persistent short runs come from hours the provider cannot supply, conflicts that stay unresolved, and history that begins inside the window. Research tuning datasets and deferred shadow windows are the main case of the last: they read market publications from at most `warmup_bars_max` + 2 = 302 h before their first decision ([protocol.py](https://github.com/MentorAliu/adaptive_spot_lab/blob/44b9a8a/services/engine/app/research/protocol.py) `WARMUP`, [datasets.py](https://github.com/MentorAliu/adaptive_spot_lab/blob/44b9a8a/services/engine/app/research/datasets.py)).

CALCULATION (arithmetic rechecked in this delivery): EMA(n) at run length L still carries ((n−1)/(n+1))^(L−n) of its seed.

| Run length (bars) | 100 | 150 | 200 | 300 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seed share in EMA(50) | 13.5% | 1.8% | 0.25% | 0.0045% |

A candidate's EMA(200) with the shortest permitted warm-up of 200 bars is exactly its seed at the first bar a `GT` or `LT` condition evaluates (a `CROSS_*` condition also needs the previous value, so it first evaluates one bar later), and it still carries about 36.8% of the seed at the envelope's 300-bar maximum. ATR(14)'s seed share is (13/14)^(L−15), about 0.18% at 100 bars. These are properties of the arithmetic, not measured effects on signals or trades.

### Placebo control for entries

For the futures trader, [ExecPlan 0045](plans/0045-where-the-results-come-from.md) (approved 2026-10-27) versions this analysis; the text below is unchanged.

PROPOSED for M8's agent-value report: replay the same entry opportunities the agent was shown, accepting each one at random at the agent's observed acceptance rate, over many recorded seeds, with the same recorded quotes, cost scenario and fill rules. Compare the agent run with that null distribution of after-cost results. This tests whether the agent's selection beats chance selection from the same opportunities. `AGENT_POLICY_V1` does not enter on a crossover ([STRATEGIES](contracts/STRATEGIES.md#agent_policy_v1-template--to-complete-before-m7-activation)), so an opportunity is a completed decision on a decision bar where the policy permitted an entry: entry evidence present, inside the action limits and cooldowns, including approved-candidate signals. A random entry takes sizing and mandate terms from the agent's own recorded choices, and it occupies the position and counts against the action limits as the policy would, so later opportunities follow from the replay, not from the agent's run. The population, the sizing and mandate rule, the seed count and the statistic are declared before the evaluation. It is a labeled analysis on isolated replays with modeled fills, never a ledger entry or forward result, and it does not amend `EVALUATION_PROTOCOL_V2`. One builder's "placebo" was a time-shifted fake signal, a different control ([TB-16](../REFERENCES.md#trading-bot-builder-and-llm-trading-research--2026-10-17)).

### Exit what-if

For the futures trader, [ExecPlan 0045](plans/0045-where-the-results-come-from.md) (approved 2026-10-27) versions this analysis; the text below is unchanged.

PROPOSED: for each closed agent-driven position that ended in an agent exit or had its stop tightened, compute what its original mandate alone (the mandate's stop and its hold/time exit) would have produced from that point, plus its maximum favorable and adverse excursion while held. Use the same recorded quotes, costs and fill rules, and report paired differences across all such positions with their count, not selected examples. Label it COUNTERFACTUAL and MODELED FILLS, and INCOMPLETE where quotes are missing. It is never a ledger entry, a forward result or a reason to change a mandate. Studies disagree on whether LLM agents sell winners early and hold losers: one simulated market reports a pronounced disposition effect, while a bias survey finds the opposite direction on average and an experimental-market study finds near-fundamental trading ([TB-09–TB-11](../REFERENCES.md#trading-bot-builder-and-llm-trading-research--2026-10-17)). This measures whether such a pattern appears here.

## Future M9/M10 agent quality and release evidence

**PROPOSED — 2026-10-16; not implemented or an amendment to an approved protocol hash.** This section owns the additional evaluation contract for [M9](plans/0027-m9-market-intelligence-and-event-driven-trading.md) and [M10](plans/0028-m10-coin-discovery-and-controlled-expansion.md). Keep three conclusions separate: **software/control acceptance**, **agent task quality and incremental value**, and **financial performance evidence**. A passed capability probe or valid abstention can prove a workflow works, but cannot establish predictive value. A favorable paper return cannot excuse a permission or accounting failure. Engineering quality has no automatic “5/5” certification.

### Frozen cases, graders and repeated trials

Freeze each case's available evidence and revisions, clock, permissions, initial portfolio/workflow state, supported tools, expected observable outcome, grading rubric and timeout/budget. Use separate development and held-out sets with provenance; do not tune on final labels, later prices or their summaries. Test constructive success as well as denial. Existing logged failures are useful cases after redaction; unknown truth remains unknown rather than an invented label.

| Case family | Required positive and negative distinctions |
|---|---|
| Evidence interpretation | Supported/unsupported claims, authentic announcements with/without demonstrated token benefit, contradictory/stale/corrected sources, ambiguous identities and plausible but unproven causal explanations. |
| Investigation and restraint | Missing evidence that a permitted search can resolve; sufficient evidence that needs no search; unavailable evidence requiring abstention. Repetition or extra calls are not success. |
| Decisions and feasibility | Permitted action versus justified hold/abstention; adequate versus exhausted/stale capacity; actionable versus expired information. Grade policy/evidence correctness separately from the later price outcome. |
| Evaluator usefulness | Correct and deliberately defective drafts, including fluent but unsupported claims and valid concise claims. Record erroneous acceptance, erroneous refusal, unresolved/abstaining reviews and the concrete defect found or missed. |
| Lifecycle and trust | Corrections, revoked grants, superseded work, worker loss, malformed/injected tool results and retries with an unknown outcome. Check actual stored state/effects and not the agent's assertion of success. |

Use deterministic checks for identities, units, timestamps, authority, calculations and effects. Use explicit model-assisted rubrics only for interpretive judgments and calibrate them against sampled human review, including grader disagreements and ambiguous cases. Human calibration is a release/evaluation activity, not a per-trade approval requirement. Preserve model/prompt/rubric versions and label provenance. Another model or a second role is not automatically an independent judge; ordering, verbosity and shared-model biases must be tested where applicable.

Run repeated trials under the frozen trial-count/sample-adequacy rule, retaining every attempt, refusal, timeout and infrastructure failure. Reset task state between independent trials; distinguish deliberate within-workflow memory from cross-trial leakage. Report counts, denominators, uncertainty and repeatability rather than selecting the best retry. Separate model errors from harness/grader defects; correct a defective case transparently and rerun affected comparisons. Grade valid outcomes and essential security transitions, not an unnecessarily fixed sequence of tool calls. Partial credit for diagnosis must never hide a failed financial or authority invariant.

### Role value, discovery coverage and selection

Use the existing isolated comparison harness; do not require a hosted evaluation service, new judge provider or orchestration SDK. Compare actual-model workflows as well as synthetic mechanics. Declare the factor intentionally varied and match the remaining available evidence, universe, market period, contributions, costs, risk/execution constraints and **total permitted inference/research work**. Report actual calls/steps/tokens when observable, tool work, latency, exposure and missing usage. Extra source coverage, extra coins, extra calls or fewer trading constraints must not masquerade as a specialization benefit.

Run targeted removal/feature comparisons when they answer a predeclared question: analyst follow-up, researcher evidence, evaluator review or discovery specialization. An isolated variant can omit review to measure its contribution; the main policy's required review remains binding. Prefer a simpler permitted workflow when the extra component fails its predefined usefulness/cost criteria; keep the last verified approved workflow until any necessary policy change is approved. Do not auto-promote a variant, change models/prompts or widen risk from an evaluation summary. Negative and insufficient-evidence findings are valid reports, but do not pass an unmet activation or quality threshold.

For discovery, measure the cheap screen and the specialist separately. Retain inclusion/rejection/unavailable reasons and audit a bounded, predeclared sample of rejected or unsurfaced candidates from a permitted reference set. Otherwise a specialist can appear accurate while the screen hides every missed candidate. Report coverage denominators and selection bias; an unobserved asset is not a true negative. Out-of-universe data remains subject to research/source grants, and later price appreciation alone is not a label of a tradable opportunity. Preserve inactive/delisted identities and distinguish specialist benefit from access to a broader universe.

## What “prove itself” means here

The product must distinguish software correctness, execution-model fidelity, strategy evidence, adaptive-selection benefit and AI incremental benefit. Positive simulated P&L alone does not establish any of these. No fixed return target, universal trade count, Sharpe cutoff or deployment date is invented in this pack.

A forward experiment can produce negative or inconclusive evidence. Passing a paper gate is not permission for live trading. Any future live program needs separate scope, funding authorization, account/legal availability checks and execution controls.

## Experiment modes and immutable manifests

Each run records mode, dataset/snapshot hashes, code commit, strategy/selector/risk/cost versions, model identifier and effective settings, prompt/schema version, external-flow schedule, provider/freshness rules, random seed where used, evaluation protocol, timestamps and known limitations. Deterministic replay uses recorded model outputs and observations, not a fresh model request.

Keep synthetic correctness tests, historical research, M6 deferred shadow replays, future live shadows and main forward-paper results separate. Never combine their balance curves or label a historical backtest a forward result. Retain every trial and rejected variant. M6 uses recorded quotes only; bar-history research is out of scope.

## Historical protocol

Use chronological development/calibration/validation periods with a genuinely untouched final evaluation segment. Account for feature warm-up, overlapping position horizons, revisions and leakage across boundaries. Time-series validation documentation explains why random folds can train on the future [EV-02](../REFERENCES.md#ev-02); a generic splitter alone does not solve trading-specific leakage.

Fit scalers, selectors, parameter choices and any regime models only on permitted development observations. Purge/embargo overlapping labeled horizons where the methodology requires it. Predeclare the complete candidate search space and selection process. If results are consulted to modify the system, those observations are now development evidence, not an untouched test.

Historical model knowledge is a separate contamination risk: an LLM may know later events even when retrieved inputs are point-in-time. Label that limitation and avoid treating historical AI performance as decisive. Forward proposals timestamped before outcomes provide cleaner evidence of the actual deployed decision process.

M6's `SHADOW — deferred replay` freezes a candidate before the observations it trades, but is not a live engine. Every result states zero processing delay, reduced eligibility without feed/lease/clock checks, and omitted research-service downtime. Its comparison is the reference replayed over the same window and rule, never the pilot's forward results. Record marks/trades and portable prefix fingerprints in the main catalog; a changed prefix suspends the shadow. Its engine mode remains `HISTORICAL_RESEARCH` until boundary work adds a live shadow mode.

As implemented in B4 and decided by the owner on 2026-10-04: one advance replays the frozen candidate and the reference strategy under `H1` only, from the freeze to the newest staged publication, and stops at every earlier mark to reproduce it. `H2_STRESS` is not run in shadows and each mark says so. A mark records both subjects' hourly marks, trade lists and portable fingerprints, the gaps of its window and whether the protocol's own minimums (168 paired hours, 10 closed positions) were reached. Reaching them makes the candidate `EVIDENCE_READY`; it is a sample-size condition, not a return threshold, a promotion or evidence of an edge. The isolated B4 runs used synthetic fixture rows and every real replay in them reports an insufficient sample.

M6's hashed protocol, partitions, scenario sets, sample minimums and promotion evidence are specified in [ExecPlan 0023 §5.5](plans/0023-m6-research-investigations.md#55-protocol-envelope-and-holdout). They reuse `EVALUATION_PROTOCOL_V1` without new return thresholds. Thin data is "insufficient sample". Holdout reuse is counted and shown; M6 applies no statistical correction. A pending promotion is not G5 success or owner approval.

## Metrics and uncertainty

Report contributed capital, equity, realized/unrealized P&L, fees/execution costs, unitized return, maximum drawdown, time in market, turnover, holding duration, trade distribution, rejected signals, data gaps and downtime. Report AI job counts/latency/failures and operating costs separately from exchange-trading P&L.

Subscription usage is not necessarily measurable as per-call dollars. Show observed usage and an optional owner-approved cost allocation; unknown cost stays unknown. Distinguish after-trading-cost results from all-in operating results. Quote units are virtual USDT unless an explicitly sourced FX conversion is added.

Use serial-dependence-aware uncertainty methods, selected and documented before final evaluation. Avoid treating every correlated trade/bar as independent. Block bootstrap or another method may be proposed during research, but block length, confidence level and assumptions must be justified and recorded. Sharpe/annualized values need return frequency, risk-free convention, dependence caveats and sample-length disclosures. Do not annualize a short run into a promised income rate.

Compare paired return streams for strategy versus baseline and AI versus non-AI, rather than just comparing two headline ending balances. Report sensitivity to costs, delay, missing intervals and parameter changes. Keep best-case/worst-case scenarios clearly distinct from measured outcomes.

## Forward protocol and freeze

Before a forward run: approve denomination/risk/cost/strategy settings; freeze the evaluation protocol and benchmark policy; record the start manifest; confirm data/ledger/model health. Every decision arrives before its modeled execution/outcome and carries evidence IDs. Approved adaptation can continue only within the already frozen adaptation rules.

When new logic is introduced, start a versioned segment/experiment with explicit change attribution. Do not stitch the winners from several changing policies into an apparently continuous successful strategy. Owner interventions and manual pauses are events affecting evaluation, not removed outliers.

## Agent-driven workflow evaluation

Extend the experiment manifest with operating/research-envelope hashes, role prompts, tool catalog/schemas, source registry, trigger/review policy, exact model-visible artifacts and their availability, action/turnover/work budgets, permission epoch and actual provider version visibility. A decision record includes tool/research delay before its possible fill. Research or model latency cannot be retroactively removed from the execution timestamp.

Required paired comparisons distinguish: fixed reference; deterministic adaptive selector; a single coordinator with the same permitted evidence and constraints; and the full analyst/researcher/evaluator/coordinator workflow. *M7 (M7-OD5 a):* the comparison is built between isolated runs on fixtures for the full workflow, the single-coordinator variant and the reference; the selector and forward ablations wait for a plan of their own after M7-B. Where feasible add feature/source/research/reviewer ablations, without removing hard risk controls. Run ablation trading in isolated portfolios; a test of reviewer contribution does not silently waive the main policy's reviewer requirement.

Measure net trading results and uncertainty as before, plus source-grounding/contradiction errors, unsupported facts, invalid/rejected intents, expired decisions, step/tool counts, incomplete workflows, latency, CPU/storage use, quota failures and owner interventions. These operational measures do not become proof of trading edge. A safer abstention rate can change exposure; compare risk/exposure-matched results rather than assume more activity is better.

The evaluator is a model role with separate context, not an independent oracle. Same-model agreement is not independent statistical evidence. Verify metrics directly from financial records; record review false acceptances/rejections in labeled tests and compare actual forward outcomes without fitting the benchmark after seeing results.

Adaptive research creates repeated testing and selection effects. Register all trial attempts and data consulted, keep final holdouts inaccessible to tuning agents, and never let a researcher access final results through a summary/report bypass. New evidence or a model/prompt/source/policy revision creates a new evaluation segment. The approved `EVALUATION_PROTOCOL_V1`, which the M4 pilot runs under, implements this for a change of code identity only. *For agent-driven runs, `EVALUATION_PROTOCOL_V2` ([below](#m7-evaluation-protocol-v2-approved-contract-execplan-0026)) names the causes:* the code identity, the runtime profile, an approved source version, the research envelope and the set of approved candidates; another policy version is another experiment. A new evidence item of an already approved source version is not a cause: it changes no approved source version (settled in the protocol's document by ExecPlan 0026 B2; the owner approves its hash). Avoid stitching only successful segments or retrospectively attributing a discretionary decision to a different strategy.

Before claiming that the full workflow is better, specify outcome measure, comparison, uncertainty method, cost treatment, observation/sample adequacy rule and acceptance criteria. No universal number of weeks, trials or profit percentage is set by this pack. A capability-complete platform may still show no incremental agent benefit; that is a legitimate report, not permission to widen risk.

Implementation status and verification are recorded in [ExecPlan 0015](plans/0015-evaluation-fixed-pilot.md).

- **Grid and returns.** The grid is hourly UTC after activation. Point 0 is the `STARTUP` valuation at activation. Each grid point is the first `HOURLY` valuation captured in the first step at or after the boundary. A missing or invalid point, or one captured more than 300 s after its boundary, is a gap. The run-relative return is the last valid NAV divided by the start NAV, minus one (gaps included). Paired samples are hourly log-return differences taken only where the strategy and the baseline both have two consecutive valid points.
- **Metrics.** The list above, plus:
  - run maximum drawdown, shown separately from the account's drawdown against its persistent high-water mark;
  - realized and unrealized P&L rebuilt from immutable fills with the ledger's own rules;
  - fees per asset, with base-asset fees also valued at their fill price as a labeled calculation;
  - costs split into the spread up to the touch and the slippage and tick beyond it (commission separate; nothing double-counted);
  - time in market; turnover = gross filled notional / mean valid hourly equity; holding durations; trade outcomes;
  - signal outcomes and rejection reasons; planned versus realized loss; gaps, downtime and owner interventions.
- **Uncertainty.** A stationary block bootstrap (Politis and Romano 1994, *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 89(428):1303–1313) of the mean paired difference: mean block 24 h with 6 h and 72 h sensitivity, 2,000 resamples, a 90% nearest-rank percentile interval, and a seed equal to the first 64 bits of SHA-256(manifest hash | protocol hash | baseline id | block). Only `random.Random(seed).random()` is used, the one sequence the Python documentation guarantees stays the same for a seed across versions. Floats appear only in these statistics. Intervals need at least 168 paired hours and trade statistics at least 10 closed positions; below that the report says "insufficient sample". There is no annualization, no Sharpe ratio and no acceptance threshold.
- **Baselines.** B0 cash, B1 100% BTC, B2 100% SOL, B3 50/50 and B4 25% BTC / 25% SOL / 50% cash (the notional caps). All are buy-and-hold and never rebalanced. Each starts with the strategy's equity and units at activation, so its NAV starts at the strategy's start NAV. A posted top-up after activation first issues baseline units at the pre-flow baseline NAV (the flow is NAV-neutral), then buys the weights. Purchases use FLOW marks (the first usable quote at or after the flow time plus the run's arrival delay): ask × (1 + slippage) rounded up to the tick, quantity floored to the lot step, commission charged in the received asset. Holdings are marked at the HOURLY marks' bid in exact Decimal. Displayed-quantity participation is not applied, a labeled idealization.
- **Reports.** `evaluation_reports` are append-only canonical JSON with SHA-256, a pure function of the manifest, protocol, as-of time and the rows bounded by it. The one extension is the hourly capture window: the grid point for hour H is the first `HOURLY` valuation and marks captured in [H, H + 300 s], so a report as of H reads hourly captures up to H + 300 s and nothing later. The evaluation worker (identity `asl_evaluation`, which may only insert reports) writes a SNAPSHOT at HH:05 as of HH:00, a DAILY report at 00:10 UTC as of 00:00, and the FINAL report once the run is STOPPED. It generates a report only after the execution checkpoint has passed that bound (as-of plus 300 s), so a later regeneration reads the same rows. A failure writes a FAILURE record and a log line; the prior report stays and nothing financial changes. Each report labels `MODELED FILLS`, `SAMPLED_L1_TICKER`, the cost-scenario label, the evidence acquisitions and the code-identity segments, and carries a limitations section.

## M7 evaluation protocol V2 (approved contract, ExecPlan 0026)

**PROPOSED.** The design is [ExecPlan 0026 §5.11](plans/0026-m7-agent-driven-main-decisions.md#511-evaluation-segments-and-comparisons). `EVALUATION_PROTOCOL_V2` is V1 with two additions and applies to agent-driven runs only; the pilot keeps V1. The owner approves its exact hash. *B2 (2026-10-11):* its document is `EVALUATION_PROTOCOL_V2` in `services/engine/app/execution/definitions.py`, V1's fields with a new identity, the segment rule below and the added measures, and an agent-driven draft names it in its protocol slot. It is not installed anywhere yet (the install command refuses until B5). Its segments and measures are computed since B6b (below).

- **Segments.** A segment starts when the code identity changes, as in V1, and when the runtime profile, an approved source version, the research envelope or the set of approved candidates changes. Each submission carries one context key over those four, and the engine records a segment at the first submission whose key differs from the last.
- **Measures added.** Decisions by action, state and reason; review verdicts and the claims reviews marked unsupported; time from trigger to submission, to the engine's answer and to the fill; model steps and tool calls per decision; expired, invalid and withdrawn intents; incomplete workflows; login and quota failures; pauses and owner interventions; the action limits and their use. None of them is evidence of a trading edge.
- **The forward control after the pilot** is one deferred replay of the reference over the agent run's window, labeled as a shadow and never shown as a forward result (built in B6c, below).
- **What B6b built (2026-10-12).** IMPLEMENTED and tested in isolation only ([plan §18, B6b](plans/0026-m7-agent-driven-main-decisions.md#b6b--segments-decision-measures-the-single-coordinator-variant-and-the-comparison-2026-10-12)); nothing is installed or switched on.
  - *Segments.* The policy engine writes `SEGMENT_STARTED` (actor `SYSTEM`, reason `CONTEXT_KEY_CHANGED`) when it answers a live run's submission whose context key differs from that of the run's submission before it, in submission order, answered or not; the run's first submission starts none. An entry that waits is answered after later submissions, so the order of answers does not decide. The event names both keys and the submission and carries the code identity on, so V1's code-identity segments still read the latest event's. A report's `segments` list holds `ACTIVATED` and every `SEGMENT_STARTED`. The reference engine never writes one. Which changes make a new context key is the dispatcher's (B5); the engine only compares keys.
  - *Decision measures.* A report under a protocol that names `decision_measures` (V2) carries a `decision_measures` section with the nine measures under the protocol's names, as of the report's time; a V1 report is unchanged, byte for byte. They are computed from the run's rows through the `evaluation_read` views ([DATA_CONTRACTS](contracts/DATA_CONTRACTS.md#m7-agent-driven-decision-records-approved-contract-execplan-0026)): codes, states, counts and times, never a document's text: a value that is not a code, and a tool name outside the catalog, counts as `UNRECOGNIZED` (the view already hides a name the dispatcher refused as `UNKNOWN_TOOL`), and an engine code qualified by a reason (`INSTRUMENT_INELIGIBLE:<reason>`) counts by its code. Times are count, minimum, lower median and maximum in milliseconds; nothing is averaged. A workflow's state counts only once it finished within the report; until then it is `OPEN`. A run's workflows are its decisions (linked by their snapshot or their trigger), their children and the outcome reviews of its positions; briefs and probes are no run's. A run without a decision workflow (a scripted intent stream) shows `NOT_RECORDED` for every value an agent workflow records, never zero. The limits section shows the policy's limits and the peak count in any window of their length, and refusals by limit code.
  - *Comparison of two isolated runs* (`app/evaluation/comparison.py`, schema 1). A pure function of two `FINAL` reports and the protocols they were generated under. It refuses, naming every reason, unless both reports are what their hashes say, `FINAL`, of fixture runs (`OFFLINE_TEST`; a research replay names no fixture), under the protocol they name, of the variants their replay blocks name, different variants and runs, on one named fixture (the reports' `replay.fixture_sha256`, which the report's generator is given rather than computing it from stored rows), one grid, one start, the same contributions, cost scenario and baseline set, and the same uncertainty and sample rules. Its paired series is the first run's minus the second's hourly log return of run-relative NAV, where both have consecutive valid points; the uncertainty is the protocol's stationary bootstrap and minimums, seeded from both manifests, both protocols and the block. Each side carries its costs, trades, signals, segments, downtime, interventions, incidents, latches, limitations and decision measures. There is no threshold: it describes and selects nothing.
  - *The single-coordinator variant* `AGENT_POLICY_V1_SOLO` ([STRATEGIES](contracts/STRATEGIES.md#agent_policy_v1-as-built-execplan-0026-b2-proposed)) and *the fixture.* Three `OFFLINE_TEST` runs on one labeled synthetic market (176 hourly grid points after 130 warm-up bars): the reference, `AGENT_POLICY_V1` whose intents a script writes as the brief, the coordinator and the intent review would, and the variant whose script is the coordinator's rule alone. With scripted decisions the three comparisons show that the comparison works, not that a role helps ([TESTING](TESTING.md#m7-b6b-segments-measures-and-the-comparison)).
  - *Not built yet:* the deterministic adaptive selector of A35 (after M7, on fixtures only), and showing any of this to the owner (B7, B8).
- **What B6c built (2026-10-12): the forward control.** IMPLEMENTED and tested in isolation only ([plan §18, B6c](plans/0026-m7-agent-driven-main-decisions.md#b6c--the-control-replay-2026-10-12)); nothing is installed or switched on, and it does nothing until research is active and an agent-driven run has been activated on the main account.
  - *What it is.* A deferred replay of `TREND_REFERENCE_V1` alone over the agent run's window, by the research runner on recorded quotes, labeled `CONTROL — deferred replay of the reference, not a forward result` with a shadow's labels and `CONTROL_REPLAY_OF_THE_REFERENCE`, `NOT_A_FORWARD_RESULT` and `EACH_PERIOD_STARTS_FLAT`. It carries a shadow's three advantages over live execution (zero processing delay, reduced eligibility, research downtime omitted) and its cost scenario, `H1`. A comparison with the agent run must evaluate the run's window under the same scenario; B6c compares nothing.
  - *Periods.* The window is replayed in consecutive periods of 21 days, a shadow's longest window: period *n* is frozen at the activation plus *n* × 21 days, ends 21 days later or at the run's end, starts flat and starts after the period before it ended. The periods together are therefore not one run of the reference, and each is short beside the protocol's sample minimums.
  - *Marks.* Each daily advance replays the period from its freeze; every earlier mark must reproduce its prefix fingerprint, or the period is suspended. A mark records the reference's figures as a shadow's mark does for its reference, and in the tests they equal a shadow's reference marks over the same window. A period ends `SUCCEEDED` when its window is replayed to its end, and `SUSPENDED` on a prefix mismatch. It ends `INCOMPLETE` when its rows or store are gone, when nothing in its window was published, when its store began too late to hold its freeze (a period that started weeks after its freeze), or when it was not finished 7 days after its window's end. `SUCCEEDED` says the window was replayed whole, not that the reference reached the protocol's sample; the mark carries the reference's status.
  - *Not shown.* No owner or agent read names it, and nothing serves a period or a mark in M7; it is never a forward result and never a trial.

## Where the results come from (ExecPlan 0045)

IMPLEMENTED with migration `0027` ([ExecPlan 0045 §4](plans/0045-where-the-results-come-from.md#4-design); `RESULT_CHECKS_V1` in [DATA_CONTRACTS](contracts/DATA_CONTRACTS.md#migration-0027_result_checks-execplan-0045)). Four checks in code, once a UTC day from 00:20 UTC, on the trader's entries since its current template's first decision, each modeled from its decision's 15-minute bar on 1-minute candles with `FUTURES_COST_V1`: against 1,000 seeded sets of random entries on the same days with the same exits (the luck floor), against the 29 other exits of generation 10's grid (one pick on the first 30 entries, checked on the later ones with a stationary bootstrap), each trade's fees, slippage and funding as a share of its risk, and Binance's taker buy share of each bar against what followed. Every reading is hindsight on modeled trades, labeled `PAPER`, `MODELED ON 1-MINUTE CANDLES`, `HINDSIGHT` and `NOTHING HERE CHANGES A RULE`; each says **too few** under its minimum (30 entries, 30 later entries, 30 UTC days). None of them is evidence of a trading edge by itself, none reaches an agent, and a change to the trader from a reading would be its own ExecPlan ([§9](plans/0045-where-the-results-come-from.md#9-implementation-sequence)). The calibration row shows how far the candle model sits from the book's fills.
