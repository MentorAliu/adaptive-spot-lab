## Approved design and invariants

- Bootstrap requires a one-time local setup secret and owner-chosen passphrase. Store only an Argon2 hash. Owner login issues an opaque, revocable, expiring DB-backed session; BFF keeps its token in an HttpOnly SameSite cookie. Require service identity plus owner session at the private API. Check Host/Origin and CSRF for browser mutation paths, and bound inputs and login attempts.
- A single immutable account has denomination virtual USDT and starts unfunded. Initialization is an explicit authenticated owner confirmation, posts 10,000.00 once, issues 100 units at NAV base 100, and remains idempotent through restarts and unknown responses.
- Top-up uses a reviewed draft with account/version/amount/denomination and canonical hash, then an owner confirmation with a durable idempotency key. Same key and payload replay the original request; conflicting reuse rejects. Any later retry explicitly targets a pending request. A flat quote-only account has a valid valuation. Missing valid holding marks produce `PENDING_VALUATION` without spendable cash; pending can be retried or canceled, with races serialized by account locking.
- Journal entries are append-only, double-entry and balanced per asset. Use Python Decimal and PostgreSQL NUMERIC, scale-8 funding amounts and scale-36 units/NAV with explicit half-even rounding and residual accounting. Cash and reserved projections are checked against the journal; available cash cannot become negative. Account version and a fencing epoch guard financial writers. Audit and outbox rows commit with financial effects.
- At a valid pre-flow valuation issue `D / NAV_before` units in the same transaction as the contribution. A contribution never creates P&L, changes high-water NAV, clears safety latches, or invents a market mark. Reconciliation fails closed on a discrepancy; startup must not silently repair it.
