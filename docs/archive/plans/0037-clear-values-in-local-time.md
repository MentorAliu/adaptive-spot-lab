## 2. What the web has today

`OBSERVED` at `9c8ee3f`:
- **Times** are written in UTC on every page by `formatUtc` (`src/lib/display.ts`: "Oct 22, 2026, 11:58 UTC"), and `timeText` adds seconds; headers and labels say "(UTC)" ("Drafted (UTC)", "Expires (UTC)", "Missing and conflicting intervals (UTC)"). The owner is in Budapest (UTC+2 until 25 October 2026, then UTC+1), so each time reads two hours off the PC's clock.
- **Decimals** are the API's strings, grouped, with redundant trailing zeros trimmed (`formatDecimal`); `decimalText` cuts a value with more than eight decimals and adds "…" ("154.64477561… USDT"). So a price reads "90 USDT" next to "64,123.456 USDT", a quantity "0.00093", a balance "10,123.45678901", and a P&L carries no sign.
- **Percentages** are computed three ways through floating point (`Number(fraction) * 100` with `toFixed` to two or three decimals) in `limits.tsx`, `agent-limits.tsx`, `report-view.tsx` and `coins-page.tsx`.
- **Charts:** Lightweight Charts writes its time axis and crosshair in UTC, and the price axis with its own default precision.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 One format per kind of value

`src/lib/display.ts`, display only. Each function takes the API's exact decimal string and formats it with `Intl.NumberFormat`, which since ECMA-402's NumberFormat v3 (ES2023) rounds a decimal string exactly, without a float in between (§6); a value that is not a plain decimal string is shown as the API sent it. `src/execution/parts.tsx` adds the units (`moneyText`, `priceText`, `quantityText`, `amountText`, `navText`, `percentText`).

| Kind | Format | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| USDT amounts (balances, P&L, fees, notional, planned loss, limits in USDT) | Grouped, to the cent; an amount under 0.10 keeps two significant digits, so a small fee does not read 0.00; a gain or loss carries its sign, and one that rounds to zero reads 0.00, never -0.00 | 10,000.00 · +12.30 · -4.05 · 0.00012 |
| A coin's price in USDT | Five significant digits and at least two decimals, so a column of one coin's prices keeps one length; trailing zeros to four significant digits | 64,123.46 · 145.25 · 2.4568 · 0.16789 · 90.00 |
| Coin quantities | Exact: every nonzero digit, no trailing zeros, with the coin | 0.00093 BTC · 12,345.67890001 SOL |
| NAV and units | Four decimals, the exact value on hover | 1.0235 |
| Percentages | From the exact fraction, two decimals (one for the Coins page's changes, up to four where a limit is finer); a change is signed; a difference of two percentages is in percentage points | 1.23% · 0.125% · +1.23% · +1.2 pts |
| Counts, large volumes, sizes, delays, lengths of time | Grouped counts; volumes in short form with the exact value on hover; sizes in KiB to TiB; delays in ms or s; durations in s, min, h and d | 1,234,567 · 90.1M · 12.5 KiB · 120 ms · 4 min 05 s · 3 d 4 h |

Two tables keep exact digits, padded to the longest value's decimals so the decimal points line up (`formatExact`, `placesOf`): the candle table, which is M2's precision-preserving alternative to the chart ([FRONTEND](../contracts/FRONTEND.md#routes-and-functional-surfaces)), with its volume headed by the coin ("Volume (BTC)"), and the top-ups, which the owner confirms to the digit. Home's balances read to the cent with the exact figure on hover.

### 4.2 Times in the viewer's zone

- **The format.** Every instant on the pages is written in the viewer's time zone as "22 Oct, 13:58:04", or to the minute where seconds do not matter (schedules, retries, the chart's crosshair, the markets' hours); the year is added outside the current year ("22 Oct 2025, 13:58:04"). The text is built from `Intl.DateTimeFormat`'s numeric parts (24-hour clock) and fixed English month names, so the server and the browser write the same text whatever their locale data.
- **`DateTime`** (`src/components/date-time.tsx`) writes a `<time dateTime>` element whose hover text gives the full time with the zone's offset and the same instant in UTC: "22 Oct 2026, 13:58:04 Budapest (UTC+2) · 22 Oct 2026, 11:58:04 UTC". A missing time reads "Not recorded" (or the page's own word, such as "Not started"), an unreadable one "Unknown". `useTimeText` gives the same text inside a sentence.
- **Which zone.** The browser's own (`Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`), which on the owner's PC is the zone Windows is set to. The sidebar's footer, and the page's footer on a phone, say which: "Times in Budapest time". Summer and winter time follow the zone (Budapest moves from UTC+2 to UTC+1 at 01:00 UTC on 25 October 2026).
- **Server rendering.** TanStack Start's Hydration Errors guide: pick a deterministic zone on the server from a cookie, UTC until the browser has sent one (§6). The root route's loader reads the zone through an isomorphic function (`src/lib/time-zone.functions.ts`): on the server the `asl_time_zone` cookie, accepted only when this runtime knows it as an IANA zone, else UTC; in the browser its own zone. `TimeZoneProvider` reads it with React's `useSyncExternalStore`: hydration uses the server's zone, so the text matches, then the browser's own zone takes over, and an effect writes the cookie (`Path=/; Max-Age=31536000; SameSite=Lax`). From the second page load on, the server writes the owner's zone from the start.
- **Rules that are UTC stay UTC, shown in local time.** The daily loss limit resets when the UTC day ends: Home and Settings give that moment on the owner's clock ("at 02:00 Budapest time"). The coin scan's schedule reads "02:20 Budapest time (00:20 UTC)". The daily reference names its "UTC day". Column headers drop "(UTC)".

### 4.3 Charts

Lightweight Charts 5.2.1 has no time zone option; its official skill says to format with `Intl.DateTimeFormat` in `timeScale.tickMarkFormatter` and `localization.timeFormatter` (§6). `chartTime(zone)` (`src/lib/chart-theme.ts`) gives both from the display functions above (year, month, day, time or time with seconds by the tick's `TickMarkType`; the crosshair to the minute), and the chart applies them again if the zone changes. The bars keep their UTC timestamps, and the library places its ticks by them (OBSERVED in the installed `dist/lightweight-charts.development.mjs`: `weightByTime` compares the UTC year, month and day and UTC-aligned hours), so only the labels are local: in Budapest a new day's tick stands at 01:00 or 02:00 local time and names that day. The candle chart's price axis and crosshair use the price format: as many decimals as the closes need (`pricePlaces`), with `minMove` 10 to the minus that number.

### 4.4 The pages

- **Home:** the balances to the cent with the exact figure on hover, P&L signed, holdings and positions with their coin, the check time and the decisions' times in local time.
- **Trades:** positions and orders with prices, quantities with their coin, fees, P&L and planned loss in their formats; stops as prices; every time local; a position's and an order's detail likewise.
- **Decisions:** the drafts' times, sizes, prices, modeled fills and the trigger results in their formats; the agents' limits as signed USDT and exact percentages.
- **Markets:** quotes as prices, quantities with their coin, the feed's times and ages, delays and sizes; the candle table exact and aligned; the chart local. **Coins:** volumes in short form with the exact value on hover, changes as signed percentages to one decimal, the change against BTC in percentage points, the scan's time and schedule local.
- **Settings:** the limits' figures and bars, the drawdown and the daily reference, the run's times, the agents' usage and the health table in their formats.

## 7. Data and trust-boundary changes

One cookie, `asl_time_zone`, written by the page's script and read by the web server: it holds an IANA zone name such as `Europe/Budapest`, nothing about the account. The server accepts it only when it is at most 64 characters of letters, digits, `_`, `+`, `-` and `/` and this runtime knows it as a zone; anything else means UTC. It is not `HttpOnly`, because the browser writes it. The API, its routes and its data are unchanged.

## 8. Failure and rollback

A web change that misbehaves is fixed forward with the next update; no data depends on how a value is written. Without the cookie, or with an unknown zone, the pages read in UTC, as before this plan, and the hover text names UTC. The update file's own backup and rollback tags apply as for any update.
