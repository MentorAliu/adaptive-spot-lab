The thread's answer, as the project's systematic trader ([TRADING](../../TRADING.md)): the app's rules all look at hours or one week and pay fees at that pace; the one family of rules with a century of evidence is slow trend-following sized by volatility ([TR-01](../../REFERENCES.md#trading-research--2026-11-01-and-2026-11-02), [TR-03](../../REFERENCES.md#trading-research--2026-11-01-and-2026-11-02)), which the app does not run. ## 4. Design

### 4.1 The rule `TREND_SLOW` (code, in `TWIN_SET_V3`)

Every value is `PROPOSED`, from the sources named, fixed here before the first bar and never tuned on a result.

| Part | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| When | At the first finished 15-minute bar of each UTC day the twin reads, from the day after it started (as `MOMENTUM_1W`); a day not readable yet (closes missing) is read again at the day's next bar, with one note a day; a fill, or a reading that changes nothing, spends the day | 0041 §4.1 |
| The closes | Binance's 1-day candles of the perpetual (§4.2), each UTC day's close; `D` is the last complete UTC day before the bar's day | §4.2 |
| The direction | `s` = the mean of three signs: the sign of `close(D) − close(D−30)`, of `close(D) − close(D−91)` and of `close(D) − close(D−365)` (calendar days; a sign is 0 when equal). An anchor day absent from the store is replaced by the nearest earlier stored day within 7 days; none within 7 days, and the day is not readable | TR-03: 1-, 3- and 12-month trend signals, equally weighted |
| The volatility | `σ` = the annualized exponentially weighted standard deviation of the daily log returns over the stored days from `D−365` to `D`: `var ← λ·var + (1−λ)·r²` with `λ = 60/61` (a centre of mass of 60 days), seeded with the first return's square; a return across a gap of missing days counts once; `σ = sqrt(365 · var)`; fewer than 300 of the 366 days stored, and the day is not readable | TR-01: a 60-day exponentially weighted volatility and a 40% target |
| The target | `f = s · min(2, 0.40 / σ)`, quantized toward zero to 0.01: the fraction of equity to hold as notional, positive long, negative short; `|f| < 0.05` is flat (0) | TR-01, TR-03; 2 is the policy's leverage cap |
| Acting | `held` = the open position's notional at the fresh mark over the twin's equity, signed by its side (0 when flat). When `|f − held| > 0.25`: the open position is closed (`SIGNAL_EXIT`) and, when `f ≠ 0`, the target is entered in the same step on the book the exit left (its own side's levels when the exit took them); otherwise nothing changes. The day is spent by the fill (`RESIZED`, `FLIPPED`, `ENTERED`, `EXITED`) or the reading (`NO_CHANGE`). An exit that cannot fill (a book too thin for it, or a fill beyond the liquidation price without a fresh mark) is not left to the protection's retry: nothing fills, and the day's next bar reads again and tries the whole resize again. An entry refused after its exit filled is owed: the day's next bars enter the target although, flat, it lies inside the buffer (the reading's reason `ENTRY_OWED`) | The turnover buffer of §4.7's check; a close-and-open resize, the whole retry and the owed entry, §13 |
| Size | Notional = `|f|` × equity at the fill; leverage 1 when `|f| ≤ 1`, 2 above (both the policy's); margin = notional / leverage; the entry fee, the exit fee at the entry price with the slippage and the policy's funding reserve must fit the free wallet, as `size_whole` requires; the instrument's steps and minimums apply; a refusal (a stale quote or mark, a thin book, a size under the minimum) tries again at the day's next bar | 0041 §4.1 |
| Exits | Only a resize, a flip, an exit to flat or a liquidation; no stop, no take profit, no time exit, no latch (as `HOLD` and `MOMENTUM_1W`); after a liquidation, the next UTC day's reading | 0041 §4.1 |
| Costs | Both taker fees, the slippage scenario, funding paid and received every 8 hours, and liquidation at the mark, all `FUTURES_COST_V1`'s on the same quotes and marks as the trader's | 0041 §4.1 |

The rule's arithmetic is exact Decimal (`app.futures.twins.slow_trend`): the logarithms and the square root are the decimal context's, so two readings of the same closes agree to the digit, and a reading is stored with its closes' days, the three signs, `σ`, `f` and `held` (the `DAY_SIGNAL` event and the candidate's `signal`), so the thread can recompute every day from the stored candles (§4.6).

### 4.2 The daily candles (the feed, code)

A new table `perp_daily_bars` (migration `0038`): one row per UTC day of the BTCUSDT perpetual, `open_ms` aligned to the day, open, high, low, close, volume, quote volume and trades, the source `REST`, when it was received; append-only (migration 0019's `asl_perp_append_only`): a stored day never changes or goes. Only final days are stored: a day is final when its close time lies at least a minute before the provider's clock's lower bound at the answer (the 1-minute history's rule, with a minute's margin: a stored day never changes and is read for a year, so its last trades must be in, §13).

A new feed job, `daily` (`app.perp.daily`, run by `PerpFeed` beside `history`): once the provider's clock is measured, at the start and then 90 seconds after each UTC midnight, and while a day of the last 400 is missing again after 10 minutes (after an hour when the provider returned nothing new), it asks `GET /fapi/v1/klines` with `interval=1d` from the first missing day to now, `limit` 400 (weight 2, [REF-F4](../../REFERENCES.md)), stores the final rows it lacks, and notes `DAILY_STORED` (the count) or `DAILY_FAILED` (the reason). A pass that falls in the 90 seconds after a midnight (the job's start, or a retry) is followed by one at their end, when the day that midnight closed is final, whatever it found lacking. A day the provider does not return after it closed is asked again at the next pass (no `absent` set: a daily gap would starve the rule, so it is never given up). Until migration `0038` is applied the job waits and says so once; it asks the database again, and a failed request is asked again, after the feed's 30 seconds. The request shares the client's weight guard with the rest of the feed: one request a day once the 400 days are stored.

Why REST days and not the app's own 1-minute candles: the rule needs 366 days and the store keeps seven; the 1-day candle's close is the day's last trade, the same price the 1-minute candle of 23:59 closes at, so one source serves every day the same way. The twin reads the stored days only; it never asks the provider itself.

### 4.6 The measure, pre-registered

The opponent is holding Bitcoin: the `HOLD` twin, on the same quotes, costs and funding, over the window both twins share. The statistic is the paired daily log-return difference, `TREND_SLOW` minus `HOLD`, by the league's rule for a counted day (`app.futures.league`), with the league's stationary bootstrap (mean block 3 days, 2,000 resamples, seeded from `TWIN_SET_V3`'s hash and the two twins' ids), 95%. `BEATS_HOLD` when the interval is above zero, `TRAILS_HOLD` below, `UNCLEAR` otherwise; "too few" under 30 paired days. Read by the weekly review (read-only SQL on the stored valuations), first after 90 days, then quarterly; the league's own row against the trader reads beside it. Expectation written now: `UNCLEAR` for at least the first year (`CALCULATED`: §4.7's difference of about +10% a year had a 95% interval of about ±45% a year over 4.8 years; one year widens it). A second reading, descriptive: the twin's worst fall against `HOLD`'s over the same window.

The consistency check, code's: each `DAY_SIGNAL`'s `s`, `σ` and `f` equal `twins.slow_trend` recomputed on the stored daily candles of that day (`tests/test_m11_slow_trend_unit.py` on fixed closes; the weekly review on the PC's rows).

Trial ledger: the two offline trials of §4.7 (long-or-short, long-or-flat) and this twin, the long-or-short arm live. A `BEATS_HOLD` is evidence for a later plan; nothing changes from a verdict, and the trader's own test ([0053 §4.5](0053-the-trader-every-four-hours-and-on-events.md#45-the-measure-pre-registered)) is not reset by this plan.

### 4.8 The trading review ([TRADING.md](../../TRADING.md#how-to-judge-a-trading-change)'s eight questions)

1. **Rules:** paper only; code keeps every exit, size and limit; no stop loosened, no hold extended, no cap raised, no model; the owner's ledger untouched. Passes.
2. **Costs:** one to two resizes a week at about half the wallet, each a round trip of 0.12% (`CALCULATED`: 1,951 of fees over 4.8 years, about 2.6% a year of the start equity); funding about 2,000 over the window, paid when long with the crowd. The gain in §4.7 is after both.
3. **Horizon:** signals of 1 to 12 months, holds of weeks: the horizon the evidence is at (principle 4).
4. **The model's place:** none. The rule is code.
5. **The yardstick:** `HOLD`, paired by day, §4.6; "too few" and then `UNCLEAR` for a long time, said in advance.
6. **Pre-registration:** §4.1 and §4.6, with the thread's `SPEC.md` of 19:19 UTC before the first number.
7. **The trial ledger:** three (§4.6).
8. **The clock:** the trader's test is not reset (a twin; nothing the trader sees changes). Cost: no model call; one REST request of weight 2 a few times a day; one small table.

## 11. Failure and rollback

A missing or late daily candle: the twin notes `DAY_INCOMPLETE` once a day and reads again at each bar; no fill without the closes, no close guessed. A failed daily request: `DAILY_FAILED`, retried at the next pass; the feed's other jobs go on. A stale quote or mark, a thin book: a refusal, retried at the day's next bar (a resize whose exit cannot fill is tried whole again then); the open position keeps its liquidation check every second. The twin's fault: that twin alone stops and shows in Settings. The update's rollback is the standard one (the backup restored, the previous images); the migration's downgrade refuses once the twin has records, as every twin migration does.
