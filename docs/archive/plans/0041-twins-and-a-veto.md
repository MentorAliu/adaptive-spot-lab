## 1. Goal

The owner, 2026-10-25 07:22 UTC, in the project thread "Find an AI agent trading edge": "On everything so far related to trading crypto or stocks in general, is there a possible way to use ai agents to create an edge, think of this as an inventor and be creative".

The thread answered with the brainstorm "Where AI agents could earn a trading edge" (a Claude Doc, [summarized in docs/ideas](../ideas/agent-trading-edges.md)): seventeen ideas ranked by how plausible a measurable after-fee gain is, by what each costs on the shared Codex allowance, and by how each is tested on paper. Its verdict: an agent is unlikely to beat Bitcoin by guessing the next 15-minute move (BTC's median 15-minute move over 30 days was 0.074%, below the 0.10% a round trip costs in taker fees, [0039 §15](0039-bitcoin-futures-on-paper.md#15-progress)); where it can help is in saying no to code's trades, reading slow text, sizing by the market's structure and running honest tests. Its recommended first experiment was "twins and a veto". At 07:36:24 UTC the owner picked **"Plan it now, build after the coach"** on the card "Plan 'twins and a veto' as the next experiment?".

Observable outcome:
1. Beside the trader's account, three **isolated paper accounts (the twins)** trade the simulated BTCUSDT perpetual on the same bars, with the same 10,000 virtual USDT, the same limits, the same costs and the same fill model, each by a fixed rule: hold BTC, a trend rule, and the trend rule with a cost gate and a funding filter. Cash is the zero line and needs no account.
2. Home shows the **league table**: for each account the net P&L after fees and funding, the trades, the fees paid, the maximum drawdown and the time in market, and the paired daily difference between the trader and each twin with its interval, labeled **too few** until the declared minimum sample.
3. In update 2, a fourth twin takes the trend rule's entries only when a **veto agent** does not refuse them. The agent answers GO, HALVE or VETO with a reason from a fixed list and cannot start a trade; silence is GO. Code compares that twin with the plain trend twin and with random verdicts at the agent's own rates (the placebo control [EVALUATION](../EVALUATION.md#placebo-control-for-entries) has declared since 2026-10-17).
4. Nothing of the owner's account, its ledger, the policy, the limits, the protection or the trader changes. The twins can be removed without touching any of them.

What it will probably show, said before it runs: over 30 days hold-BTC or the gated trend twin most likely leads; the trader's trades cluster near zero after fees; the veto's effect is small and inside the noise at 60 candidates, so update 2 runs longer. A result either way is the finding; nothing here promotes, adapts or changes a rule.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The twins (code)

`TWIN_SET_V1` is a code-owned document (`app.futures.twins`, kind `FUTURES_TWINS`) seeded into `execution_definitions` with its hash, as `FUTURES_POLICY_V1` is; the worker starts only when the stored row equals the code, and a twin set records the hash as `SYSTEM`. A new set is a new version in an update the owner installs.

| Twin | Rule | Values |
|---|---|---|
| `HOLD` | Enters long once, at the first finished 15-minute bar after the set starts, and holds. No stop, no take-profit, no time exit. After a liquidation it never re-enters | Leverage 1; the whole wallet as notional, less the entry fee and the funding reserve. Funding is paid or received every 8 hours as the trader's is, so this is holding BTC through the perpetual, not spot |
| `TREND` | At each finished 15-minute bar, from the newest contiguous complete 1-hour candles built from final 1-minute candles: enter long when the 20-period EMA crosses above the 50-period EMA, enter short when it crosses below; exit on the opposite cross. `TREND_REFERENCE_V1`'s arithmetic, warm-up 100 hours, with a short side | Leverage 1; at most 0.25% of equity at risk at the stop, fees and slippage included, as the policy sizes it; stop 2.0 × the 15-minute ATR(14); no take-profit; time exit 24 hours; the policy's cooldowns, 24 entries a UTC day, daily loss and drawdown latches |
| `TREND_GATED` | `TREND`, plus two refusals. **The cost gate:** no entry whose stop distance per unit is below 3 × the round trip's cost per unit (two taker fees on the entry price, the slippage scenario twice, and the quote's spread). **The funding filter:** no entry on the paying side while the announced funding rate's magnitude is above 0.03% (three times Binance's default of 0.01%) | As `TREND` |
| `TREND_VETO` (part 2) | `TREND`'s rule; each entry that passes every check and is sized is held, and the veto agent is asked first (§4.3) | As `TREND` |
| `MOMENTUM_1W` (part 2) | At the first finished 15-minute bar of each UTC day the twin reads, from the day after it started (the day it starts, that day's first bar came before it): long if the last complete UTC day's close is above the close of the day 8 days before it, short if below, no change if equal. A day's close is its last complete 1-hour candle's, built from final 1-minute candles, and a day counts only with its 24 hours complete; until both days are complete (the warm-up, or minutes the feed may still fill) the twin reads again at the day's next bar, with one note a day. Flat, it enters the signal's side; on the other side it exits (`SIGNAL_EXIT`) and enters the signal's side in the same step, on the levels of the book the exit left; on the same side it holds. It exits only on a flip or a liquidation; after a liquidation, the next UTC day's signal. After the app was down over midnight it reads at the first bar it reads that day | As `HOLD`: leverage 1, the whole wallet less the entry fee, the exit fee and the policy's funding reserve, no stop, take-profit or time exit, no latch; funding and liquidation as the trader's. A fill or a reading that changes nothing spends the day; a day not complete yet and an operational refusal (a stale quote, a short book) try again at the day's next bar |

Every twin uses the trader's own money functions on the same inputs: `size_entry`, the fill that walks the five levels of a quote at most 2 s old plus the slippage scenario, the fee, funding and liquidation arithmetic of `FUTURES_COST_V1`, and the stop on the bid for a long and the ask for a short. Without a fresh quote there is no fill: an entry is refused and an exit waits, as for the trader (rule 11). The engine's fenced step protects each twin's position every second (liquidation, stop, time exit) after the owner's position and before the valuation, whatever the agents or the run's state.

**Records** (migration `0023`, [§13](#13-decision-log)): `futures_twins` (the twin, the set's hash, the wallet, the high-water NAV, the state `RUNNING`, `LATCHED`, `STOPPED` or `FAULTED`, and the segment it belongs to); `futures_twin_positions`, `futures_twin_fills` (the walked levels and the quote's second copied in, since quotes are deleted after 7 days), `futures_twin_funding`, `futures_twin_valuations` (every 60 s, as the trader's); and `futures_twin_candidates`: every bar at which a twin's rule signaled an entry, with the quote's five levels, the ATR, the stop, the size, the gate's and the filter's verdicts and the funding rate, so a later replay has what it needs after the quotes are gone. The database guards of `futures_positions` apply (a stop only tightens, a closed position never changes).

**Not the owner's ledger.** The twins post nothing to the journal, hold no reservation and never write `account_state`: they are isolated paper accounts, as M4's baselines and M7's isolated runs were, labeled so. Each twin's wallet is recomputed each hour from its own rows (10,000 + realized P&L − fees ± funding − liquidations, exact Decimal); a difference marks the twin `FAULTED`, stops it and shows in Settings. Reconciliation of the owner's ledger is unchanged.

**`TWIN_SET_V2`** (part 2, migration `0030`, hash `ff907a4a…2c29`) is the set in force from update 2. It carries `TWIN_SET_V1`'s three twins with the same rules and values: a carried twin goes on in the segment it started under V1, with its history, its trades and its comparison's seeds; the two new twins start segment 1 under V2 as `SYSTEM`'s at the update's first twin step. The momentum twin's day is recorded on its row (`rule_day`) with the day's reading as an event (`DAY_SIGNAL`), and its entries are candidates with the rule `DAY_MOMENTUM`.

**Segments.** A twin set runs as one segment from the bar it starts at; a new set version, a latch that stops a twin or a fault ends that twin's segment, and the table says so. The trader's segments are its runs, as today. Days on which the feed was down for the trader or a twin are left out of the paired differences and counted.

### 4.2 The league table (code)

Computed by code in exact Decimal from each segment's start, for the trader and each twin: net P&L after fees and funding, the return on the starting 10,000, trades, fees paid, funding paid and received, maximum drawdown from the account's own high-water NAV, time in market; and BTC's own price change over the same window as a plain number beside them. For each twin, the paired daily differences of the trader's and the twin's daily log returns of NAV, with a 95% interval from `app.evaluation.bootstrap`'s stationary bootstrap (mean block 3 days, 2,000 resamples, seeded from the set's hash and the segment's id, so the number is reproducible). A comparison is labeled **too few** until both have 30 days and the trader has 60 closed trades in the window; the minimum, the statistic and the seeds are these, fixed here before the first bar.

Labels on the table and the API: `PAPER`, `MODELED FILLS`, `ISOLATED TWINS`, `THE TWINS ARE NOT THE OWNER'S ACCOUNT`, `NO THRESHOLD: NOTHING IS PROMOTED OR CHANGED BY THIS TABLE`.

### 4.3 The veto agent (update 2): `ENTRY_VETO_V1` (EVALUATOR), generation 12

A fourth twin, `TREND_VETO`, with `TREND`'s rule and values, whose entries pass the agent first.

- **Started by** SYSTEM when `TREND_VETO`'s rule signals an entry: one at a time, at most 24 a UTC day (the entries cap), a 120-second deadline. The candidate (side, stop, size, the gate's and filter's verdicts labeled as code's) is held meanwhile; a GO or HALVE then fills at the newest quote after the answer, at most 5 minutes after the bar (the policy's decision age), and the fill records the price's move from the candidate's quote, so the cost of asking is visible.
- **Shown:** the context the trader would see at that bar, built by `app.agents.futures_context` with the twin's account and position in place of the owner's, the candidate, and the reason list `VETO_REASONS_V1`. No tools. One model step, one retry after a refusal.
- **Answers** with its FINISH, an `ENTRY_VETO` artifact:

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| `target` | The candidate's ID and hash |
| `verdict` | `GO`, `HALVE`, `VETO` |
| `reason` | One of `VETO_REASONS_V1`: `RELEASE_WITHIN_2H`, `FUNDING_EXTREME`, `SPREAD_WIDE`, `FEED_GAP`, `HEADLINES_CONTRADICT`, `TREND_WEAK`, `NONE` |
| `note` | One line, at most 160 characters |
| `confidence` | `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH` |

- **Code** checks the schema (an unknown field is refused), the target and the reason. `GO` enters as the rule says; `HALVE` enters with half the risk fraction (0.125%); `VETO` records the skipped candidate. **Silence is GO:** a deadline passed, a refused answer after its retry, a cancel or a model refused for the usage limit lets the entry go ahead as the rule says, recorded `NO_ANSWER`, so the twin never waits on the model past the deadline and the table keeps the agent's answers apart from its absences.
- **The slot** goes to the trader first, as [0040 §4.6](0040-trade-reviews-and-a-coach.md#46-when-they-run-and-what-they-cost): a veto call starts only when no trader decision is queued or open, and a decision queued meanwhile cancels it at the runner (then `NO_ANSWER`). Calls: at most 24 a UTC day, each one model step with no tools, at `MODEL_TABLE_V1`'s EVALUATOR pair; with the trend rule's actual signal rate, 5 to 20 a day are expected.
- **As built (part 2).** The twin's step writes the held entry as a request (`futures_twin_veto_requests`: the candidate as planned at the asked quote, its hash, the asked time and a deadline 120 s later), one per bar and one open at a time. SYSTEM starts the agent for the oldest open request whose deadline is at least 30 s away, once per request, while no trader decision is queued or open and under the day's 24; the call yields to a trader decision queued meanwhile (`YIELDED_TO_DECISION`), runs at most until the request's deadline (its budget: two model steps, 110 s a step, 115 s in all), and is exempt from the HH:59:30 to HH:06 quiet window, since the twin's deadline does not wait; no reviewer or coach starts while a veto may. The agent sees the request, the twin's account and the trader's market blocks as of its start (the policy's summary, the market, the features with `trend_1h`, the candles, the headlines and the releases), never the owner's account, run, position, limits, trades, decisions, note or scores. Its answer is checked against the bound request and written as one `futures_twin_vetoes` row in the artifact's transaction, stamped with the database's time; the twin applies it only when recorded by the deadline, at the next second. `VETO` spends the hour's cross; `GO`, `HALVE` and silence are checked and sized again on the newest quote and filled; a stale quote or mark waits a second at a time until the bar is the policy's decision age old, and is then refused `BAR_TOO_OLD` (not spent, so the next bar of the hour asks again). A twin that stops or faults with a request open leaves it unapplied (`TWIN_ENDED` on the page). The fill records what asking cost: the fill price against the price planned at the asked quote, per unit and in bps, positive when worse for the side.
- **The placebo** (code, daily): for every candidate `TREND_VETO` saw in the window, 1,000 seeded random verdicts drawn at the agent's observed `GO`, `HALVE` and `VETO` rates, replayed through the same sizing and a **1-minute-candle fill model**: the entry at the candidate's stored quote with the slippage scenario, the stop or the time exit on the candles (a stop filled at the stop price with the slippage against it; a candle that touches both the stop and a cross counts the stop), funding from the settled rates. As built: each candidate resolved by the twin is replayed once its 24 hours are final, from the first whole minute at or after it was asked, at the price planned at the asked quote; the stop at its level, or at a gapped minute's open, with the slippage against it; the opposite relation of the 1-hour EMAs at the next hour's open; the 24-hour time exit; both taker fees and each settled funding the hold crossed. The result is in R (the net over the full-risk entry's loss at the stop with both fees and the slippage), weighted by the verdict (`GO` and `NO_ANSWER` 1, `HALVE` 0.5, `VETO` 0): the veto arm takes the agent's verdicts, the trend arm `GO` for every candidate, and each of the 1,000 streams draws a verdict for each answered candidate at the agent's own `GO`, `HALVE` and `VETO` rates (a `NO_ANSWER` stays `GO`), seeded from the set's hash, the twin's ID and the newest candidate. The agent worker writes one result a UTC day from 00:40 (`futures_twin_placebo`), only while no trader decision is queued or open; a replay missing a minute or a rate waits and is tried again 15 minutes later. The veto twin's own candidates are replayed through the same candle model for this comparison, so both sides use one fill model; the twin's live per-second books are shown beside it, labeled as the finer model. **Success, fixed here:** the veto arm beats `TREND` after costs with the paired interval of §4.2 excluding zero, and its candle-model P&L beats 95% of the 1,000 seeds, at 60 candidates seen at least; otherwise the finding is that the veto adds nothing. Code applies the rule and prints the outcome; no words decide it.

### 4.5 The Scorecard's longer rows (part 2, folded in on 2026-11-02)

The Decisions page's scorecard judges the trader's direction calls again at the 4-hour and 24-hour checkpoints the scorer already stores (ExecPlan 0043), with the same 0.12% band and the same coin-flip line. Calls 15 minutes apart share most of a longer horizon, so a row's bootstrap block is the horizon's calls (16 and 96, against the hour's 4) and a row needs the hour's 30 right and wrong calls times its hours (120 and 720); the seeds are the hour's with the checkpoint added. The rows are for the page only: no agent's context reads them (a test reads every agent module), so the trader's context, generation and the paired check due 2026-11-16 are unchanged.

## 5. Authority

[AUTONOMY_MATRIX](../../AUTONOMY_MATRIX.md), [AGENT_TOOL_CONTRACTS](../AGENT_TOOL_CONTRACTS.md) and [RUNTIME_AGENTS](../RUNTIME_AGENTS.md) change with the code of each part:
- **The twins** are `SYSTEM`'s, by `TWIN_SET_V1`. Nobody changes a twin's rule or values at run time; a new set is an update the owner installs. A twin's latch is the policy's; a twin's fault stops that twin alone.
- **`MOMENTUM_1W`** is `SYSTEM`'s by `TWIN_SET_V2`, as the other twins are; nobody changes its rule at run time.
- **`ENTRY_VETO_V1`** (EVALUATOR) finishes only with its own artifact; no tool; started by SYSTEM, never by a model; it can only refuse or halve `TREND_VETO`'s entry, in that twin's isolated account, and never start one, exit one, touch the owner's account, the trader, a run, a limit or a policy. The matrix's row "critique an artifact with no financial effect on the owner's account" covers it; one row names the twin it binds.
- **The trader is unchanged:** `FUTURES_POLICY_V1`, or `FUTURES_POLICY_V2` once 0040 is installed, generation 7 or 8, the risk gate and code's protection stay as they are. The table changes no rule ([AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) rule 4); a rule changes only in an update the owner installs, after its own ExecPlan.
- **Rule 13's money-path review.** The owner's ledger, the journal, the reservations and `account_state` do not change, so the review is not required by the rule. The twins' books get an independent review all the same, because the comparison rests on them, and the reviewer checks that no twin code can reach the owner's rows.
