## 4. Design

### 4.1 The run

### 4.2 The simulated world

Built minute by minute on the archive, in the live order (`MODELED` where §4.10 says):
- **The decisions** come from generation 14's schedule: at each finished 15-minute bar while the run is running or a position is open, `futures_schedule.due` on the inputs `futures_schedule.read` takes live, built from the archive and the account (the complete hours and quarters, the last decision's reference price, the 15-minute ATR, the funding rates, the last closed position, flat or not), at most 32 a UTC day. The context is built 5 s after the close; the answer applies at the next minute's open; an unanswered step is a `HOLD` that says so (0052).
- **The context** is `futures_context.build` with `coached`, `scored` and `lean` and the schedule block, composed by the live code's own functions from the simulation's records instead of the database (§4.3), with the live size rule (headlines, releases, then scores dropped above 36,864 bytes). The recent decisions show their trigger kinds.
- **The scorer** scores every decision and every finished 15-minute bar from the window's start at 15, 60, 240 and 1,440 minutes with `decision_scores`' own build (`DECISION_SCORING_V2`) on the archive's candles and settled funding rates. A score is recorded 20 s after its checkpoint (live: within a pass of 15 s once the candle is final), so a decision never sees a score whose checkpoint is its own bar's close; a checkpoint without data is stored `INCOMPLETE` seven days on, as live.
- **The fact sheet** of each closed trade is built a minute after its close with `trade_facts`' sections, its feed items empty (§4.10).
- **The trade reviewer, the decision reviewer and the coach** run as live: due by the live rules (a waiting sheet, then a due day with a case from 01:30 UTC, then the coach after the newest unanswered review), one at a time in that order, 3 starts a record; bound to the live blocks (`trade_review`, `decision_review`, `trade_coach`) built by the live code; their answers checked by the live finish rules and recorded with code's fields (the grid, the counts, the held confidence, the expiry) as live. Their timing is §4.5.

### 4.3 The live code, not a copy

Every live function the simulation needs that reads the database is split into its read and a pure part that composes the result from rows; the live function keeps its name, arguments and output and calls the pure part, and the simulation calls the same pure part with its own rows. Each extraction has an equivalence test (§6, E1 to E3): the pure part on rows a database fixture holds gives the live function's output on that fixture, byte for byte, and the live modules' existing suites pass unchanged. The parts, `PROPOSED` (the build may split differently and records it in §12): `futures_schedule.shown`; `futures_context._coach`; `score_context`'s lean block, coach rows, scored count and day card; `trade_records`' review states, recent reviews, scoreboard, reviewed count and note in force; `trade_facts`' sheet sections; `decision_scores`' inputs from minutes and rates; `decision_reviews`' cases; and the three `_prepare_*` finish rules, split into the checks against the bound block and the record code writes. Where the simulation must supply what a table holds (an account row, a position, a decision and its answer), it writes the same fields the live reads select.

Every key of every block a model sees is classified (a price, a quantity, a time, a date or neither), and a key with no class fails the leak test (E5). ### 4.5 The feedback agents' timing (`MODELED`)

A reviewer, decision reviewer or coach step starts at the first instant on a 15-second grid at or after it is due at which the live gate holds in simulated time: outside HH:59:30 to HH:06:00; not within a trader decision, taken as open from its bar's close to 60 s after; 30 s after that; and at least 300 s plus 60 s before the next 15-minute bar. Each takes 60 s; its record is in force from its end, so the trader sees a review or note from its next decision. The sheet is due a minute after the close; the decision review from 01:30 UTC for the day before, once the day's decisions have their 1-hour scores; the coach once no review waits. `CALCULATED` with this rule: the review of a trade closed at 10:07 starts at 10:08 and the coach's note after it is in force at 10:10, before the 10:15 decision; the review of one closed at 10:13 starts after the 10:15 bar (at 10:16:30 if a decision ran then), so a decision at 10:15 would show it `PENDING`.

### 4.7 The design guard

The answer to "not using the old one again": the run is tied to the live design by code, not by a promise.
- **The fingerprint** (`LIVE_DESIGN_GEN14_V1`), frozen in generation 18's definitions and in `0037`: the live templates the schedulers start (`futures_trader.TRADER`, `trade_reviews.REVIEWER`, `DECISION_REVIEWER` and `COACH`) with each template's definition hash, its prompt file's hash and its step schema's hash; the hashes of `FUTURES_POLICY_V1`, `FUTURES_COST_V1`, `DECISION_SCORING_V2` and `SCHEDULE_V1`; and the SHA-256, with LF line endings, of each live module whose code the simulation calls or mirrors: `futures_context`, `futures_schedule`, `futures_trader`, `decision_scores`, `score_context`, `trade_records`, `trade_facts`, `trade_reviews`, `decision_reviews`, `app.futures.scoring`, `app.futures.scorecard`, `app.futures.score_views`, `app.futures.money` and `app.futures.engine`. (The build added the read tool and the candles' module, and the independent review the helpers, definitions and answer models they read, and the twin set's hash: 37 files in all, §12.)
- **Before any call:** the lane computes the fingerprint from the installed code before it starts each workflow, and the child before it builds the run. If it differs from the frozen one, nothing starts (`LIVE_DESIGN_CHANGED`, logged with the parts that differ), the run stays `RUNNING` with nothing lost, and the lane's note says so. A later update either brings the backtest up to the new design (a new generation, with the equivalence tests run again) or ends the run.
- **In the cloud:** a unit test asserts that the code's fingerprint equals the frozen one, so a change to any of these parts fails the suite until the backtest is checked against it.

### 4.8 The measures, pre-registered (a third trial)

On this run, computed by the report from its stored answers replayed in code (0055 §4.8 and §12), every figure `MODELED`:
- **M1, does it make money after costs?** Its mean daily log return over the window's whole UTC days; stationary bootstrap, mean block 3 days, seed `derive_seed("BACKTEST_GEN14_V1", "TWO_MONTHS", "M1")`: `MAKES_MONEY` when the pass interval's lower end is above 0, `LOSES_MONEY` when the 95% interval's upper end is below 0, otherwise `UNCLEAR` (0052 §4.8).
- **M2, against holding BTC (`HOLD`), and M3, against the gated trend rule (`TREND_GATED`):** the paired daily log-return differences, the same bootstrap and rule (`BEATS_HOLD`, `TRAILS_HOLD`, `BEATS_RULE`, `TRAILS_RULE`, `UNCLEAR`); seeds `…"M2"` and `…"M3"`.
- **M4, its calls 4 hours out against the last-4-hours rule.** At each answered trader step (an abstention or a step given up is no answer), the BTC move in percent from the context's price (`features.price`, the close of its newest complete minute) to the close of the minute ending 240 minutes after it, in the called direction (`UP` the move, `DOWN` its negative, `NO_VIEW` 0), minus the rule's (the sign of `features.returns_percent.4h`; 0 when it is 0 or missing); a step without the 4-hour close is left out and counted. Paired in time order, stationary bootstrap, mean block 8 steps, seed `…"M4"`: `PASS` when the pass interval's lower end is above 0, `WORSE` when the 95% interval's upper end is below 0, otherwise `NO_DIFFERENCE`.
- **M6, the latest design against the hourly design.** Over the whole UTC days both runs covered, this run's daily log return minus that of 0055's Claude run (`BACKTEST_AI_V1:TWO_MONTHS_CLAUDE`, finished 2026-11-06), paired by the day; stationary bootstrap, mean block 3 days, seed `derive_seed("BACKTEST_GEN14_V1", "M6")`: `LATEST_BETTER` when the pass interval's lower end is above 0, `HOURLY_BETTER` when its upper end is below 0, otherwise `UNCLEAR`. (M5 is 0055's, Claude's calls against ChatGPT's.)

**The family:** the three runs are three trials of one question, with fourteen tests: 0052's M1 to M4 (ChatGPT), 0055's M1 to M5 and this run's M1 to M4 and M6. **A pass needs 279/280 (99.64%)**, 1 − 0.05/14; a fail stays at 95%; the family's readings take 20,000 resamples (by 0055's rule, the 36th and the 19,965th of the sorted means), the first 10,000 drawing 0055's sequence from the same seeds. The earlier runs' registered readings (0052's at 98.75%, 0055's at 179/180) are printed beside their family readings, each labeled.

**Descriptive, no pass rule:** its 1-hour calls against the last-hour rule (0052's M4 at 60 minutes) and the hit rates beyond the live band (0.12%) at 1 and 4 hours with Wilson intervals; decisions a day by trigger; entries refused and why; trades, wins, mean net R, fees, funding, time in a position and exits by reason; confidence levels and reason codes; how the trader answered the coach's notes (`advice_use`); the notes' leans and confidence, and when the hold at `LOW` ended; the reviews' grids and tags; the decision reviews' labels and `knowable`; the cost per answered step by kind; the provider failures and back-offs; the months the account gained or lost; and beside the hourly run, the trades, fees and share of steps entered. **C1 and C2 are not run:** the same model and mask as 0055, which read `NO_SIGN` and `NO_LEAK_SEEN`; mask V2's leak test (E5) covers the new blocks.

## 11. Failure and rollback

- **The guard holds** (`LIVE_DESIGN_CHANGED`): nothing is spent; the thread tells the owner and either brings the backtest up to the new design in a later update or ends the run there.
- The API refuses the request itself (`REQUEST_REFUSED`, a schema it cannot compile): the lane waits an hour between tries and nothing is lost; the thread fixes it forward.
- The credit ends before the cap (`QUOTA_BLOCKED`): the lane tries once an hour; the live agents go on; the thread tells the owner.
- A data or state mismatch in the child stops the run, as 0052's.
- To stop it early: on the owner's word, a later update ends the run; removing the key on the Claude API card in Settings holds it at once (`NEEDS_KEY`).
- Rollback: the update's rollback tags bring back the previous images; `0037` only adds documents and widens checks, and its downgrade is refused once a generation-18 record exists (fix forward). The live modules' extractions change no output, so a rollback of them changes nothing live.
