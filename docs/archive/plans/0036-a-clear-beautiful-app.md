## 4. Design

### 4.1 The app shell

- **Desktop (768 px and wider):** shadcn's Sidebar, `variant="inset"`, `collapsible="icon"`, as in shadcn's `dashboard-01` block: the app's mark and name with the PAPER label at the top; the five menu items with an icon each (Home `House`, Trades `ArrowLeftRight`, Decisions `BrainCircuit`, Markets `ChartCandlestick`, Settings `Settings`) inside `<nav aria-label="Primary">`, the section shown marked with `aria-current="page"` as today; the paper notice at the bottom. Collapsed, the items show their icon with a tooltip. Ctrl/Cmd+B toggles it (shadcn's shortcut). It opens expanded on every load: the shadcn cookie is written but not read, as nothing on the server needs it.
- **Phones (narrower than 768 px):** a fixed tab bar at the bottom, the standard pattern for three to five top destinations: the menu's links in shadcn's button look (`buttonVariants`), each with its icon and its word; it is the `Primary` navigation there, so exactly one is visible at any width. The Sidebar's mobile sheet is not used.
- **Top bar** (inside the content area): the sidebar toggle (desktop); on a detail page the way back (shadcn Breadcrumb: its section, linked to the list, then the page), as a list page needs none: the menu marks its section and the title names it; the app's name on a phone; a PAPER badge that stays visible when the sidebar is collapsed and on phones; and the theme switch.
- **One `<main>`:** shadcn's `SidebarInset` is the page's only `main`; `PageShell` and the route error, pending and not-found views become plain containers.
- **Trades and Markets** keep their two addresses; their switch becomes a shadcn ButtonGroup of links in the button look, with icons (`Layers`, `ReceiptText`; `ChartCandlestick`, `Coins`), in the shell above the page's title.
- **Links stay links.** Every menu item, switch, section link and "Open …" action that looks like a button is a link styled with `buttonVariants` (and `cn`, which settles the variant's border), as the repository already did: Base UI's Button gives any element it renders `role="button"` (§11).

### 4.2 Theme

shadcn's official TanStack Start dark mode: a `ThemeProvider` whose `ScriptOnce` sets the `light` or `dark` class before React hydrates (no flash), `suppressHydrationWarning` on `<html>`, and a mode switch (shadcn DropdownMenu, Light, Dark or System, System by default) in the top bar. The colours are the preset's own `:root` and `.dark` tokens. The candle chart and the run's NAV chart read the tokens again when the theme changes and apply them with Lightweight Charts' `applyOptions`, so they follow the theme without being rebuilt. A token is read as the canvas draws it and keeps its transparency (the dark theme's borders are white at 10 %, so the grid stays faint).

The candles keep the preset's two chart colours, which differ only in shade; to make the direction readable by shape as well, a candle that closed at or above its open is hollow (outline and wick in `--chart-2`) and one that closed below it is filled (`--chart-4`), the convention of "hollow candles" (WCAG 2.2 1.4.1). The open hour's provisional bar stays grey. The last price's line and label take that grey (`--muted-foreground`) for every bar, because Lightweight Charts colours them with the last bar's fill, which a hollow candle does not have (§6).

### 4.3 Home

1. **Trading status**, the first card, unchanged in what it says: its badge gains an icon (`Activity` on, `CirclePause` on hold, `CircleOff` off, `CircleQuestionMark` unknown); each note is a shadcn Item with an icon for its kind (`Hand` Needs you, `TriangleAlert` Problem, `Info` Info), its badge, its text and its link as a Button with an arrow; "Checked" carries a clock.
2. **Account balances**, four cards in shadcn's `dashboard-01` section-cards layout (one, two or four columns by the content's width): **Account value** (the API's `equity`, with how it is valued), **P&L** (the API's `pnl`, with a Gain, Loss or Even badge and its arrow; the sign is read from the decimal string, nothing is computed), **Available** (with what is reserved for open orders) and **Contributions** (a top-up is not profit). Values keep their exact decimal strings, grouped, in tabular digits, with "virtual USDT".
3. **Paper holdings** (each coin a row with its ticker mark, quantity and reserved part) beside **Cashflow-neutral performance** (units, NAV, high-water NAV as labelled figures).
4. **Open positions** and **Latest decisions**, as today, with numbers right-aligned.

### 4.4 Shared parts (all pages)

- Every state badge is a shared `ToneBadge`: the API's word, with one of seven tones, each a badge variant and an icon: done (`CircleCheck`), working now (`Activity`), on hold (`CirclePause`), needs a look (`TriangleAlert`), failed or refused (`CircleX`, destructive), off (`CircleMinus`) and not yet under way (`CircleDashed`). Orders, positions, decision drafts, agent workflows, runs, investigations and the health table use it; `ModeBadge` and `ModeledBadge` carry their own icons (PAPER `FlaskConical`, SYNTHETIC TEST `TestTube`, REPLAY `History`, MODELED FILL `Calculator`). So a state never relies on colour alone ([Carbon's status indicators](https://carbondesignsystem.com/patterns/status-indicator-pattern/); WCAG 2.2 1.4.1).
- `EmptyState` shows an icon in shadcn's `EmptyMedia`; `ReadError` and the owner-session alert carry an icon.
- `DataTable` sets tabular digits, and a column marked numeric in TanStack Table's column meta is right-aligned, header and cells ([NN/g data tables](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/data-tables/)): quantities, prices, fees, stops, planned losses, realized P&L, limits and their use, and the Coins page's rank.
- Home, Settings and the list pages share one header: the section's icon, the title and the description. A detail page (a position, an order, a decision draft, an agent workflow, a run) keeps its link back to its list above its title and its badges, which a phone needs as the top bar's way back shows from 768 px.
- Limits are drawn as "used of allowed" bars (shadcn Progress) beside their exact figures: each risk rule's current use against its limit, the drawdown against its latch, and the agents' action counts. The bars are display only: the figures stay the API's strings, and a missing value or a zero limit draws no bar. In a list of figures the bar is its own `<dd>` below the figure.

### 4.5 Settings

The five sections keep their ids and order. On wide screens the section links stand in a column beside the sections and stay in view while scrolling; on phones they are a row at the top. Each link and section title carries its icon (`Bot`, `Play`, `Gauge`, `Wallet`, `HeartPulse`).
