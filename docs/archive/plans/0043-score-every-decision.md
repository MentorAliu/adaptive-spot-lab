# ExecPlan 0043 — Score every decision against what followed

**Status: APPROVED by the owner on 2026-10-26 at 11:51 UTC ([DECISIONS](../../DECISIONS.md#2026-10-26--score-every-decision-against-what-followed-execplan-0043)): "Yes, in two updates". Part 0 (this plan) done; update 1 installed on the PC on 2026-10-26 at 15:10 UTC and update 2 at 19:17 UTC (§14).** Written by the project thread "Score every decision against what followed" from `main` at `522e455`. A part of M11 ([ExecPlan 0039](0039-bitcoin-futures-on-paper.md)), built before [ExecPlan 0041](0041-twins-and-a-veto.md)'s part 2: code scores every decision of the trader, every 15-minute bar and every twin entry against what the price did next, a scorecard sums the scores, and then the trader, a daily decision review and the coach read them. It changes no money path, limit, protection or policy.

Labels: `OBSERVED`, `PROPOSED`, `IMPLEMENTED`, `VERIFIED`, `NOT RUN`. Every number in §4 is a `PROPOSED` engineering default, declared before any score exists and never tuned on one.

## 1. Goal

The owner, 2026-10-26 11:34 UTC, in the project thread "Score every decision against what followed": "I want functionality of app that also tracks what happened for the decision, for example if it decided to hold and price went up for the specific time, I want that to record too and let agent analyze that if it's useful, same would go if it decided to hold and price went down for the time, include all combinations, be creative and how would this help the agent or the app ?"

The thread answered with a Claude Doc, "Score every decision: proposal", and at 11:51:05 UTC the owner picked **"Yes, in two updates"** on the card "Build the decision scorecard as proposed?". The option read: "I write ExecPlan 0043 and build update 1 (the records). Update 2 (the agents learn) follows once update 1 is checked on your PC." How it helps, as the Doc put it: the agents learn every 15 minutes instead of only from closed trades (at the 07:06 UTC check the trader had held all 71 of its decisions, so 0040's reviewer and coach had nothing to learn from); the scores tell careful holding from timid holding; they catch entries that only paid fees, holds into a loss, early exits and stop tightenings that shook a position out; the trader's direction calls are checked against a coin flip; and the twins' and the veto's entries are scored within hours instead of only in the 30-day league reading.

Observable outcome:
1. For every trader decision, every finished 15-minute bar since the futures run began and every twin entry, code stores what the price did 15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours and 24 hours later and what a long and a short test trade would have netted after fees, slippage and funding, with one label per checkpoint (§4.2). Nothing is estimated; a gap is shown as a gap.
2. Decisions shows what followed each decision; Home shows the scorecard: the labels' counts, the base rate of a trade at any bar, the bars the trader missed, and (update 2) its direction calls against a coin flip.
3. In update 2, the trader reads its scored decisions and the scorecard and states the direction it expects for the next hour; a reviewer writes one decision review a day of the day's largest misses and worst decisions, graded on whether they were knowable then; the coach writes a note after it.
4. Nothing of the account, its ledger, the risk gate, the limits, the protection, the policy, the twins' rules or the trader's actions changes. No score or lesson changes a rule.

## 2. What the app has

`OBSERVED` at `522e455`:
- **Decisions.** `futures_decisions` holds each decision: its trigger (`BAR`, `FILL`, `SHARP_MOVE`), the bar, the context's time and hash, the position it saw, the action (`ENTER_LONG`, `ENTER_SHORT`, `EXIT`, `TIGHTEN_STOP`, `HOLD`, `ABSTAIN`), its choices and rationale; generation 8 adds `advice_use`; the artifact holds `reason_code`, `uncertainty_level` and `invalidation`. `futures_decision_answers` holds the engine's answer (`FILLED`, `APPLIED`, `NOTED`, `REFUSED` with codes; a tightened stop's `previous` and new `stop`). Decisions that never finished are `agent_workflows` rows without a decision. The trader is shown its last 5 actions with their answers (`futures_context._decisions`) and its last 10 trades.
- **Market data.** `perp_bars` keeps every final 1-minute candle; `perp_funding_rates` keeps the settled rates; quotes and marks are kept 7 days. `futures_context` builds the trader's features from the 1-minute candles (ATR15 by `money.atr`, the 1-hour trend by `trend_1h`).
- **0040's records.** `trade_fact_sheets` (one per closed position, built by the agent worker's scheduler pass, hashed and immutable), `TRADE_REVIEW` and `TRADE_ADVICE` artifacts, and the trade scoreboard (`trade_records.scoreboard`). The coach starts only after a new trade review.
- **0041's records.** `futures_twins`, their positions, fills, funding, valuations and `futures_twin_candidates` (every bar a twin's rule signaled, with the gate's and the funding filter's verdicts and the outcome); the league table (`app.futures.league`) with its stationary block bootstrap (`app.evaluation.bootstrap`).
- **Costs.** `FUTURES_COST_V1`: taker 0.05% a fill, the slippage scenario 1 basis point a fill, funding every 8 hours at the settled rate.
- **Measured.** BTC's median 15-minute move over 30 days was 0.074% ([0039 §15](0039-bitcoin-futures-on-paper.md#15-progress)), below a round trip's 0.12%.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The scores (code)

`DECISION_SCORING_V1` is a code-owned document (`app.futures.scoring`, kind `FUTURES_SCORING`) seeded into `execution_definitions` with its hash, as `TWIN_SET_V1` is; every score records it. A new version is an update the owner installs.

- **Subjects.** `DECISION`: every `futures_decisions` row, from its recorded time, once the engine has answered it. `BAR`: every finished 15-minute bar since the first futures run started, from the bar's close, with the decision taken for it (that of the first trader job started from the close until the next close) or why none was (that job's end, or `NO_JOB`), and whether the trader was expected to decide (the run running or a position open at the close, from the run's events). `TWIN_ENTRY`: every `futures_twin_candidates` row, from its time. The veto's verdicts are added by 0041's part 2.
- **The reference.** The close of the final 1-minute candle that ends at the start of the subject's minute (the trader's own `price` feature when the feed is current). The conditions are taken at the same time, with the trader's own code (`futures_context.features`): ATR15, the 1-hour trend (`trend_1h`), the UTC hours and the sign of the funding rate the newest mark announced.
- **Checkpoints.** 15, 60, 240 and 1,440 minutes after the subject's time; 60 is the pages' headline. A checkpoint is scored once its last 1-minute candle is final. The path is the 1-minute candles from the subject's minute to the checkpoint, one a minute. A missing candle (the reference's or the path's), or an unanswered decision, waits, since the feed asks for the missing candles of the last seven days every five minutes; once the checkpoint is seven days old, it is written `INCOMPLETE` with its reason, counted and never filled (rule 11). An unanswered decision's keeps the position the decision saw, and whether it was executed is unknown. A subject the scorer itself fails on is logged, tried again five minutes later, and written `INCOMPLETE` as `SCORE_FAILED` once seven days old.
- **The price path.** The checkpoint's close against the reference, and the highest high and lowest low on the way, in percent and in multiples of ATR15 at the reference.
- **Two test trades**, a long and a short, from the reference: leverage 1; the entry at the reference close moved against the side by the slippage scenario, with the taker fee; a stop at 1.5 × ATR15 from that entry and a target at 2 × the stop's distance (exact, not rounded to the tick: a measuring stick, not an order); exit at the stop (at a candle's open when it opens beyond the stop), the target or the checkpoint's close, with the slippage scenario against it and the taker fee; a candle that touches both the stop and the target counts the stop; funding at each funding time after the entry and at or before the exit, at the settled rate on its stored mark (a long pays a positive rate). Each gives its end (`TARGET`, `STOP`, or `CHECKPOINT` at the checkpoint's close), its net in percent of the entry's notional and R (the net over the loss at the stop with its fees and slippage). Exact Decimal; labeled `MODELED_ON_1M_CANDLES`; liquidation is not modeled, since the policy keeps every stop inside the liquidation price.
- **A held position**, for a decision that saw one still open at its time: what the position's own stop, take profit and time exit, as they stood at the decision (its initial stop and the tightenings applied before it), would have given by the checkpoint on the same candles (a time exit, `TIME_LIMIT`, at the open of the first minute at or after it), against exiting at the reference, per unit, with each exit's slippage and fee and the funding the hold crossed. A decision whose position closed before it counts as flat.
- **Records.** `futures_decision_scores` (migration `0024`): one row per subject and checkpoint, with the definition's hash, the subject's keys, the reference, the state (`SCORED`, `INCOMPLETE`), the label, the document (path, test trades, held position, conditions, the label's inputs) as canonical JSON and its SHA-256; immutable once written, as `trade_fact_sheets` are. Written by the agent identity in the agent worker's scheduler pass, as fact sheets are, oldest due first, at most 20 subjects a pass, each in a savepoint; read by the API. The passes after the install score everything since the first run started (a few thousand rows at most).

### 4.2 The labels (code)

Code sets one label per subject and checkpoint from the position the subject saw, its action and the scores; no model word sets one. "Paid" means a test trade's net was above zero. In a position, "with it" and "against it" mean the held position's result (§4.1) beyond one exit's cost (0.06% of the reference) for or against it.

| Before | Action | Long paid, short not | Short paid, long not | Neither paid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flat | `HOLD`, `ABSTAIN` | `MISSED_LONG` | `MISSED_SHORT` | `NOTHING_TO_CATCH` (`chop` when both stopped) |
| Flat | `ENTER_LONG` | `RIGHT_SIDE` | `WRONG_SIDE` | `FEES_ONLY` |
| Flat | `ENTER_SHORT` | `WRONG_SIDE` | `RIGHT_SIDE` | `FEES_ONLY` |

Both paid: `EITHER_WAY` for a wait, `RIGHT_SIDE` for an entry. A twin entry takes the entry rows. A bar's own label says which test trade paid (`LONG_PAID`, `SHORT_PAID`, `BOTH_PAID`, `NEITHER_PAID`); the scorecard reads a bar without a decision while the trader was expected to decide as a wait, with the wait's labels.

| Before | Action | With the position | Against it | Neither |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Long or short | `HOLD`, `ABSTAIN` | `GOOD_HOLD` | `HELD_INTO_LOSS` | `HOLD_FLAT` |
| Long or short | `EXIT` | `EARLY_EXIT` | `GOOD_EXIT` | `NEUTRAL_EXIT` |
| Long or short | `TIGHTEN_STOP` | `NOT_HIT` while the new stop is not touched; once touched, `SAVED` when its exit is at least as good as the old stop's outcome by the checkpoint, else `SHAKEN_OUT` | | |

An action the engine refused keeps its label with `executed: false` (a refused tightening is `NOT_APPLIED`); a twin entry the gate or the filter refused likewise.

### 4.3 The scorecard (code)

Computed by code in exact Decimal from the scores recorded by a given time, for the last 24 hours, the last 7 days and all, at each checkpoint: each label's count and share; the base rate (the share of `BAR` subjects whose long or short test trade paid, and their average net); the bars of a running run without a decision, by why, and their labels; the same by condition (the 1-hour trend, ATR15 by thirds of the window's bars, the UTC hours 00–08, 08–16 and 16–24, the funding rate's sign, and the trader's `reason_code` and `uncertainty_level`). A row of fewer than 30 subjects reads **too few**. Update 2 adds the direction calls: an `UP` call is right when the 1-hour close is above +0.12%, wrong below −0.12%; the hit rate over right and wrong calls with a 95% interval from `app.evaluation.bootstrap` (mean block 4 decisions, 2,000 resamples, seeded from the definition's hash and the window), and the same by `uncertainty_level`. Labels: `HINDSIGHT`, `MODELED_TEST_TRADES`, `NOTHING_IS_CHANGED_BY_THIS_SCORECARD`.

### 4.4 The pages (update 1)

UI work loads the shadcn skill, keeps preset `b2fms61Bqi` and reuses the preset's components; no new dependency.
- **Decisions:** under each decision, the 1-hour label and move, and the other checkpoints on demand; pending and incomplete said so.
- **Home:** a scorecard card (the 1-hour labels for 24 hours and 7 days, the base rate, the missed bars, the labels) and, on the league card, where the trader and each twin disagreed (bars where one entered and the other waited) with their 1-hour labels.
- **Settings:** in Health, the scorer's state: its definition, what it stored, what is incomplete and what is past its hour without a score.

### 4.5 The agents (update 2, generation 9)

- **The trader, `FUTURES_DECISION_V3`.** Generation 8's trader; shown its last 16 decisions with their 1-hour scores recorded before its cutoff, and the scorecard as of its cutoff, labeled `HINDSIGHT_COMPUTED_IN_CODE; NOT_A_RULE`; answers `expected_move` (`UP`, `DOWN`, `NO_VIEW`, the next hour) at every decision. Nothing else changes; the risk gate sizes and checks as before.
- **`DECISION_REVIEW_V1` (EVALUATOR).** Started by SYSTEM once a UTC day for the day before, after its 1-hour scores are in (from 01:30 UTC), with 0040's gate and yield. Shown the day's scorecard and up to six cases: the three largest misses (`MISSED_LONG`, `MISSED_SHORT` by the 1-hour test trade's net) and the three worst decisions (`WRONG_SIDE`, `FEES_ONLY`, `HELD_INTO_LOSS`, `EARLY_EXIT`, `SHAKEN_OUT` by money missed or lost), each with what the trader saw then (features, trend, reason code, uncertainty, expected move, rationale labeled as the model's). Answers a `DECISION_REVIEW` artifact: per case `knowable` (`YES`, `PARTLY`, `NO`), a tag from `DECISION_TAGS_V1`, 1 to 3 evidence paths into the case and a note of at most 160 characters; and one lesson of at most 200 characters. Code checks the schema, the cases and each path; `NOT_REVIEWED` after 48 hours. No tools; one model step, one retry after a refusal.
- **The coach, `TRADE_COACH_V2`.** `TRADE_COACH_V1`, also started after each decision review; its evidence may cite decision-review IDs and scorecard row keys, whose counts code adds; its confidence is held `LOW` while fewer than 10 trades are reviewed and fewer than 288 decisions (three days) are scored at 1 hour.
- **Calls:** one review and one note a day, with their retries 2 to 4, beside the trader's up to 120.
