## 1. Goal

The owner, 2026-10-21 06:29 UTC, in the project thread "Simpler app for trading": "why are there so many pages, I don't think I need all these pages, also why is there a research page, and what it does, I think there are to many pages that as a User I don't care to see, it makes it to complicated, I don't think this app is on research anymore hence I want the app to let it trade on it's own, make a great refactor of the app and leave only the most important parts that as a User I may want to see".

## 2. What the app has today

`OBSERVED` at `8f04b9b`:
- **The menu has 14 pages:** Account, Markets, Orders, Positions, Risk, Experiments, Research, Evidence, Coins, Decisions, Policies, Agents, Approvals, System; with their detail pages the web has 22 routes. Yet research runs: the worker queues `SCHEDULED_RESEARCH` once per newly sealed dataset and `FOLLOW_ON` when a reviewed assessment names a research question (both start `RESEARCH_V1`), starts `PROMOTION_V2` for an evidence-ready candidate and records pending promotions in force. A `RESEARCH_V1` workflow ran on the PC on 2026-10-19 and failed on the usage limit ([STATUS_HISTORY](../STATUS_HISTORY.md)): research spends the ChatGPT allowance the trading agents need.

The update file includes the `research` and `research-admin` profiles, migrates the research stores on every run and recreates the research services last.
- **What trading uses of research code:** the shared modules `protocol`, `projections`, `definitions`, `grammar`, `interpreter`, `jobs`, `stores` and `strategy` (an import walk from every service's entry point, the migrations and the update file; the runner, bridge, queue, staging, datasets, shadows, control replay and identity modules are reached only by the research services and their tests). ## 4. Design

### 4.1 Five pages

The menu: **Home, Trades, Decisions, Markets, Settings.** Each page is built from today's components, moved rather than rewritten, so their tests keep their assertions.

| Page | Shows | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** `/` | Whether the app is trading, and anything that needs the owner, each with where to act; the account's value (available, reserved, contributions, P&L, NAV, holdings); open positions; the latest decisions | New status card from the system status, the agents' runtime and the main run; the balance cards moved out of the account page; the positions and decisions queries |
| **Trades** `/positions`, `/orders` | Positions and orders, one switch between them | The two pages as they are, under one title |
| **Decisions** `/decisions` | What the agents decided and why, and what each trigger led to | The page as it is |
| **Markets** `/markets`, `/coins` | Prices and candles; the coin watchlist and notes, one switch between them | The two pages as they are, under one title |
| **Settings** `/settings` | The agents' switch, sign-in and usage limit; the main run with Pause, Resume, Flatten, Stop and a new run; limits, latches and entry pauses; virtual money (top-up, funding history) and sign-out; the health table | The switch card; the run controls of the run page; the limits of the Risk page; the funding part of the account page; the System page's table |

Detail pages stay, outside the menu: a position, an order, a decision, an agent's workflow (back to Decisions) and a run (titled "Run #N", back to Settings).

**Removed pages:** Research and its two detail pages, Evidence and its detail page, the experiments list, Approvals, Policies, the agents list, Risk and System. Their links in kept pages go, and the browser suites of removed pages go with them. A removed address answers "Page not found", like any unknown one.

**Wording:** the title and footer say paper trading, not research; nothing asks for an approval or a Start (M8). The status card says in plain words what is wrong and what the owner can do, and never claims trading when a check failed or a value is unknown (rule 11).

### 4.2 Research off in the agent worker

- The scheduler no longer queues `SCHEDULED_RESEARCH` or starts `PROMOTION_V2`, and a reviewed assessment no longer records `FOLLOW_ON` (the `ASSESSMENT_READY` trigger it also records stays: trading needs it).
- Once it holds the lease, before its first tick, the worker settles every queued `SCHEDULED_RESEARCH` and `FOLLOW_ON` trigger as canceled with reason `RESEARCH_OFF`, and ends every open workflow of the research templates (`RESEARCH_V1` and its child `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_V1`, `PROMOTION_V1`, `PROMOTION_V2`) the way a cancel ends one, with the same reason, a parent before its child. A model call in flight is canceled at the runner, and its result is stored late, as for any canceled workflow. A failure is logged and tried again at the next tick; trading work goes on meanwhile. A research trigger the scheduler meets later is canceled the same way. Research jobs already queued stay `QUEUED` in the history: only the retired runner took them.
- The in-force recorder no longer records pending promotions. It still records the definitions, the research envelope (the segment key reads it) and the source versions; the owner's own approvals and revocations of a promotion are still applied, so the recovery lever works as before.
- No definition, prompt, grant, budget or policy changes: the change only narrows what the code starts.

### 4.3 Compose and the update file

- `compose.yaml` loses `research-postgres`, `research-runner`, `research-migrate`, `research-bridge` and `research-admin`, the `research_private` network and the four research secrets. The volume `research_postgres_data` is no longer named, and stays on the PC.
- `tools/update.ps1`: the `research` and `research-admin` profiles and the research store migration go. The main database's migration still runs through `app.research.migrate` (a bounded retry on a lock timeout, not research), so that module stays. After the main services are recreated (the agent worker first ends any research work), a new stage stops and removes the containers of the retired services, each found by its exact Compose labels (project and service) and removed by id, never with `-f`; the network `<project>_research_private` is read whole before anything is removed, and removed by its id once its exact name and Compose's labels (this project, `research_private`) show it is this app's; another project's network of that name stops the update with the research services as they were. The record notes each container as it is stopped and as it is removed. A failure stops the update with the main app already installed. The verification adds that no container of a retired service and no such network remains, and the page check reads the seven list pages (`/`, `/positions`, `/orders`, `/decisions`, `/markets`, `/coins`, `/settings`). The tidy step then removes the retired services' images, as it removes any image no current service uses.
- The model test (`tools/m8/update-model.ps1`) models the retirement: containers of retired services stopped and removed by id, the stopped ones (listed only with `-a`) too, nothing else removed, other Compose projects' containers and network of the same names left alone, and a refused removal, a network that cannot be read and a container or network that remains as failures; no research migration.

### 4.4 API

- No route changes in this plan. The web stops calling the routes only the removed pages read: the research routes, `GET /v1/promotions`, the evidence and source routes, the policies reads and the agent workflow list. Its server functions for them go, and so do those of the approval writes, which no page offers; the response schemas stay with the routes. They stay, unused, until [ExecPlan 0034](0034-app-cleanup.md)'s part 3 removes them with the research modules only they and the retired services import, when it splits `main.py`; their tests move with them.
- `/v1/system/status` keeps its `research` row until then; the web no longer shows it or counts it (without the retired bridge it reads `unavailable` once the bridge's last storage observation ages out).
- Kept and used: every read the five pages and the detail pages use, the owner's control and funding routes, and the approval routes (§3). The OpenAPI file and the generated TypeScript types do not change; `pnpm run api:check` proves they still match.
