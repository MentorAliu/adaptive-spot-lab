### 4.1 The window and its data

  The accounts start at the window's first minute; history before it is visible to the rules and the trader's candles, as the feed's past is live.
- **The data:** the same files as 0051 (§4.1 there) for the window and the 30 days before (warm-up): monthly 1-minute klines and mark-price klines and monthly funding rates of the BTCUSDT perpetual from `data.binance.vision`, each with Binance's `.CHECKSUM`, daily files where a monthly file lacks days; for C1 (§4.5), the daily closes of 2025-11 to 2026-10. The instrument's filters are the live instrument row's.
- **In the app** (§13, an engineering choice before any call): the window's files ship with the update, byte for byte as Binance publishes them, in `services/engine/app/backtest_ai/archive/`: the monthly 1-minute klines and mark-price klines and the funding rates of 2026-08 to 2026-10 (31 days of warm-up before the window), each with its `.CHECKSUM`, and the instrument's `exchangeInfo` read for 0051 (about 8.4 MB, copied from 0051's download on the owner's PC). Generation 15's hashed definition lists every file's name, size and SHA-256 (`BACKTEST_ARCHIVE_V1`), so the profile binds them; the child process checks that list and every `.CHECKSUM` and parses them with 0051's strict reader before the first step, and any difference stops the run (`ARCHIVE_MISMATCH`). A minute the archive lacks stays missing (`OBSERVED` in 0051: the trade klines of 2026-10-28 and the mark of 2026-10-28 and 29 are not published); nothing is guessed. No download, no key, no sign-in, no private endpoint, no new host. C1's truths (§4.5) need the year's files, which the app does not hold: the report reads them (§4.9).

### 4.2 What the model sees (generation 15)

Four templates, purpose `BACKTEST`, started by `SYSTEM` only:

| Template | Role | Prompt | Context | Calls, two months (one year) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BACKTEST_TRADER_V1` | `COORDINATOR` | 0050's `trader-replay.no-feedback.v1.md` unchanged (v5 without the coach's note, the lessons and the scores) | the trader's context built from the simulated account, as 0050's `NO_FEEDBACK` arm shows it, masked | one each finished hour: 1,464 (8,760) |
| `BACKTEST_VETO_V1` | `EVALUATOR` | `veto.v1.md` unchanged | the veto's context of the held entry, masked | one a held entry: about 27 (160), `CALCULATED` from 0051's 0.42 to 0.46 trend candidates a day in 2026 |
| `BACKTEST_REAL_DATES_V1` | `COORDINATOR` | as `BACKTEST_TRADER_V1` | the same context as a trader step, unmasked | 250 (500) seeded hours (§4.5) |
| `BACKTEST_MEMORY_PROBE_V1` | `ANALYST` (no tool; asked only while the profile gives it the trader's model and effort) | `backtest-memory-probe.v1.md` (new, §4.5) | a real date only | 60 seeded days of 2025-11 to 2026-10 |

**The context** has the live builder's shape and blocks, filled from the simulation, never from the live tables: the trigger (`BAR`), the policy, the market (a quote from the minute's open as in §4.3, the mark, the index as the mark, the last settled funding rate and the next funding time), the features and candles computed by the live functions from the archive's minutes, the features' `trend_1h`, the account, run, position, limits, the last 10 simulated trades and the last 5 backtest decisions, and `headlines` and `releases` as `{"omitted": "UNAVAILABLE"}`; without `scores`, `advice` and each trade's `review`, exactly as 0050's `NO_FEEDBACK` arm removes them from a stored context (its own transform is applied, so the two cannot drift). The two reads answer from the same data: `query_futures_candles` from the step's stored reads (the last 96 complete candles of each of its four intervals before the step's minute, built from the archive by the live functions and masked, written with the step, so a read is the last `candles` of them); `read_headlines` with no headline and the unavailable label.

**The mask** (`BACKTEST_MASK_V1`), applied by code to everything the trader and veto templates see:
- Every time is shifted by +1,820 days (260 weeks): the weekday, the time of day and the funding hours stay; the calendar reads 2030 and 2031, after any model's training.
- Every price, ATR, tick, stop, take-profit and liquidation price is multiplied by a fixed factor `F` from `sha256("BACKTEST_MASK_V1|F")` mapped into [2.5, 3.5]; quantities are divided by `F`, so notional, margin, fees, funding amounts, equity and every percent are unchanged. `F` is in the code and this plan, never in a context; the report prints it.
- The trader's answer holds no price (actions, leverage, ATR multiples, hours, the call and its confidence), so nothing maps back.

Why 0050's no-feedback prompt and not `trader.v5.md`: history has no coach's notes and no trade lessons, and the scores would have to be rebuilt for a simulated account; 0050 answers the live trader's own stored contexts with exactly this prompt and measures, on the same contexts, how far its calls differ from the live prompt's (0050's S3 and S4). That reading is the bridge from this backtest to the live trader, and the page cites it.

**Differences from live, labeled in every result:** one decision an hour instead of every 15 minutes and none after a fill or on a sharp move (code still protects every minute); no news, coach's note, trade lessons or scores (the no-feedback prompt); dates and prices masked; fills modeled (§4.3); on Codex as live (§4.4), the same model and effort.

### 4.3 The simulated accounts (code, a child process of the agent worker)

A child process of the agent worker (`python -m app.backtest_ai.process`, at the lowest CPU priority, §13) runs the window with 0051's step order and the twins' and the account's own functions. It holds no database connection, no network, no model client and no key: it reads the shipped archive, prints each step as one line of JSON and reads each answer as one line. The agent worker writes the steps it prints and hands it the answers recorded; a model's answer reaches it only through that record.

- **The trader account** (`TRADER`): at the first minute of each hour (the 1-hour bar just finished, its last minute final) it prints a step, which the agent worker writes as a `backtest_steps` row: the step's real time, the masked context, its hash, its reads (§4.2) and the account's state hash; and waits for the answer. An answer applies at the next minute's open (`MODELED`: live, the decision fills about 15 to 30 s after the bar's close; the next minute's open is later, never earlier, so no answer uses a price it could not have seen), through the engine's own rules: `ENTER_LONG`/`ENTER_SHORT` sized by `money.size_entry` with the trader's choices and every refusal code of `enter` (entry limit, cooldowns, latches, position open); `EXIT` at the quote; `TIGHTEN_STOP` only when tighter; `HOLD` and `ABSTAIN` noted. Stops, take-profits, the time exit, liquidation, funding, the daily-loss latch and the drawdown latch run every minute, as in 0051.
- **The veto twin** (`TREND_VETO`): `TREND`'s rule, as 0051; at each entry it would hold, it writes a step with the veto's masked context, a few seconds after the rule's minute, and waits; the answer applies at the next minute's open (`MODELED`: live, the twin acts on the verdict at its next step within the 120 s deadline): `GO` enters, `HALVE` enters at half the risk fraction, `VETO` drops it and spends the cross, no answer enters, as the twin's silence rule says; each is checked and sized again at that minute, and a stale quote or mark waits until the bar is too old (`BAR_TOO_OLD`), as live.
- **Comparators, code only:** `HOLD`, `TREND`, `TREND_GATED` and `MOMENTUM_1W` on the same window and data, as in 0051.
- **The drawdown stop:** when an account's run stops on the 8% drawdown (the run's own high-water NAV), a new run starts at the next UTC midnight from the equity left, as the owner's new run would; each restart is recorded and marked on the page. Where the window would have ended at the first stop is reported too.
- **Order and restart:** the steps are strictly sequential (each answer changes the next context). The child process keeps the state in memory; when the agent worker starts it again (after an update or a restart of the PC) it rebuilds the state by replaying the stored answers from the window's start, deterministically (`OBSERVED` in the cloud: about 36 s to read the archive and 62 s for the whole window on one core, 78 MB at most); a rebuilt step whose kind, context hash or state hash differs from the stored one stops the run (`STATE_MISMATCH`).
- **Fills** as 0051 (`MODELED`): bid at the minute's open, ask one tick above, one level as deep as the largest order, the 1 bp slippage scenario on top; a stop at its level or a gapped open; no fill or valuation in a missing minute.
- The wallet check of 0051 runs at the end of every day for every account; a difference stops the run.

### 4.4 The calls: provider, pace and limits

**Who pays** (the owner's pick, 2026-11-03 09:50:45 UTC, §13): Codex with the owner's ChatGPT login, as the live agents, at the trader's model and effort (`MODEL_TABLE_V4`, `gpt-6.1-sol` at `medium`) and the veto's. No API key and no paid call. The backtest definition ships `provider` `CODEX_CLI`, recorded as `SYSTEM` with its hash, as everything in force.

**The allowance rule** (0050's, shared with the replay): a backtest call starts only while every Codex usage window reads below 50% used, from a reading at most 20 minutes old; at most 2 backtest calls a 15-minute bar and 192 a UTC day. The replay keeps its own 2 and 192, so the two together can reach 4 calls a bar and 384 a day before the 50% rule stops them (the replay thread, 2026-11-03 10:02 UTC). The other half stays for the live agents and the owner's own use: when a window passes 50% the backtest waits until it reads lower, and the live agents go on.

**The backtest lane:** on Codex the live orchestrator serves `BACKTEST` workflows under the profile in force, which composes generation 15 (§4.10); no second orchestrator and no change to the provider binding (J6). One inference slot serves all: a backtest workflow starts only under the reviewers' gate (`_trade_gate`: no decision queued or open, 30 s after the last, outside HH:59:30 to HH:06, ending 60 s before the next bar, no veto, reviewer or coach waiting) and is canceled at the runner when a decision is queued (`yield_to_decisions`); its step is asked again. It comes after the live trader, the veto, the reviewers and the coach (`PRIORITY_SQL` gives `BACKTEST` its own rank, 6 as the replay's, never the live agents' 5); with 0050's replay it takes turns, one workflow each, when both have one ready (neither queues while the newest of the two kinds is its own and the other has a step waiting); its graph joins the workflow graph's chain so the replay's `trade_start_waits` and `yield_to_decisions` still run.

**What it asks next:** the 60 probes first (C1 early, §4.5), then always the oldest step without an answer: each trader or veto step, and right after a trader step its real-dates shadow when it has one (§4.5). One backtest workflow at a time, and none while a replay is open (and the replay none while a backtest is open). **The model is fixed for the run:** the first answered step of each role pins its model and effort in the run's record; while the profile gives that role another pair, the lane waits (`MODEL_DIFFERS`), so one run never mixes models. The probe's `ANALYST` is asked only while its pair is the trader's.

**Budgets** (each template, the trader's; the veto's its own; the probe one model step and no tool): 3 model steps, 2 tool calls, 180 s, 120 s a step, one retry after a refusal; a step not answered after 3 starts is recorded `UNANSWERED` and the account goes on as if the model had answered `HOLD` (counted, never hidden).

**Calls and time** (`CALCULATED`): about 1,800 calls (1,464 trader, 250 shadow, about 27 veto, 60 probe, retries). At most 192 a day is at least 10 days; the allowance rule makes it about two weeks. At generation 11's measured 13,933 input and 291 output tokens, about 1,400 Codex credits (0.77 a call), about 16 days of the live trader's own use. The run stops at `BACKTEST_CALLS_V1`, 2,200 calls in all. **The first 100 calls measure their real share of the allowance windows** (`OBSERVED` then); if the projection passes four weeks, the thread tells the owner. The year, if the owner asks for it later (§4.1): about 9,500 calls, at this pace about 50 days.

### 4.5 Does the model remember the days?

The model's real training cutoff is not known; one audit finds effective cutoffs up to eight months before vendors' dates (TB-01). Under [EVALUATION](../EVALUATION.md#additions-proposed-on-2026-10-17-m9-r25r28-m10-r17) M9-R25 every case counts as exposed until reruns show otherwise. Hence the mask (§4.2) and two checks, declared now:
- **C1, the memory probe** (`BACKTEST_MEMORY_PROBE_V1`): for 60 days of 2025-11-01 to 2026-10-24 drawn by `sha256("BACKTEST_AI_V1|C1|" + date)` (the 60 smallest), the model is given only the real date and asked BTC's price at that day's 00:00 UTC, whether it was higher 7 days later, and how sure it is (`LOW`/`MEDIUM`/`HIGH`), with "I do not know" allowed. Measured: the 7-day direction's hit rate with its Wilson 95% interval, and the median absolute error of the stated price against the archive's price at that day's 00:00 UTC (the daily candle's open; the direction compares it with the open 7 days later), over all 60 and for the window's days alone. Outcome `REMEMBERS` when the interval's lower end is above 50% or the median error is below 3%; `NO_SIGN` otherwise. It is a label, not a gate; it also shows how far into the year the model's memory reaches.
- **C2, the real-date shadow** (`BACKTEST_REAL_DATES_V1`): for the 250 hours of the window (500 in the year) with the smallest `sha256("BACKTEST_AI_V1|C2|" + step time)` (the step's instant in milliseconds, the hour's start plus 5 s), the same context unmasked (real dates and prices, and real reads) is asked too, right after the masked step, so both arms of a pair meet the same model on the same day; its answer is recorded and never applied. A drawn hour where the trader is not asked (its run stopped, or the data missing) has no pair, so C2 has at most 250 pairs. Measured: the paired difference of the 1-hour scores (M4's score, §4.8), real minus masked, with M4's bootstrap. `LEAKS` when the 95% interval's lower end is above 0; otherwise `NO_LEAK_SEEN`. The masked arm stays the one scored in either case; a `LEAKS` says the mask was needed and is printed beside every result.

### 4.6 The veto agent

`BACKTEST_VETO_V1` answers each held entry of the simulated `TREND_VETO` twin (§4.3). History has no headlines, the veto's main input (its reason `HEADLINES_CONTRADICT`), so this measures only its reading of prices; with about 27 entries in two months it is a description, not a test; the page says so.

### 4.7 The comparators

In the same window, minutes and costs: `HOLD`, `TREND`, `TREND_GATED`, `MOMENTUM_1W` (code, as 0051), and **random entries**: 200 seeded placebo accounts that enter as often as the trader did each month, at hours drawn uniformly, once per account, among the hours the trader's account was flat and running (a drawn hour where the placebo itself holds a position or is stopped is skipped), on a fair coin's side, with the trader's own leverage, stop, take-profit and hold choices drawn from its entries, and exits by code only. Seeds `derive_seed("BACKTEST_AI_V1", "PLACEBO", i)`.

### 4.8 The measures, pre-registered

From each account's equity at every UTC midnight (its last valuation in the 65 minutes before, as the league table counts a day), on the masked run:
- **M1, does the trader make money after costs?** The mean daily log return, stationary bootstrap (`app.evaluation.bootstrap`, mean block 3 days, 2,000 resamples, seed `derive_seed("BACKTEST_AI_V1", "M1")`). `MAKES_MONEY` when the lower end of the 98.75% interval is above 0; `LOSES_MONEY` when the upper end of the 95% interval is below 0; otherwise `UNCLEAR`.
- **M2, does it beat holding BTC?** The paired daily difference, trader minus `HOLD`, the same bootstrap: `BEATS_HOLD`, `TRAILS_HOLD`, `UNCLEAR`.
- **M3, does it beat the gated trend rule** (the twin the live trader is compared with)? Trader minus `TREND_GATED`, the same: `BEATS_RULE`, `TRAILS_RULE`, `UNCLEAR`.
- **M4, are its 1-hour calls better than the last-hour rule?** At each trader step, the score is the BTC move over the next 60 minutes in the called direction (`+move` for `UP`, `−move` for `DOWN`, 0 for no view), from the archive's closes; the rule's is the same with the sign of the last hour's return (0 when 0). The mean paired difference over the steps in time order, stationary bootstrap (mean block 8 steps, 2,000 resamples, seed `"M4"`): `PASS` when the 98.75% interval's lower end is above 0, `WORSE` when the 95% interval's upper end is below 0, otherwise `NO_DIFFERENCE`.

98.75% is Bonferroni for the four measures. **Descriptive, no pass rule:** the final equity, growth, volatility, Sharpe ratio (√365), the largest fall from a high, trades, wins, mean net R, fees, funding, time in a position, refusals by code, restarts; the hit rate of the calls beyond the 0.12% band with its Wilson interval; the share of placebo accounts that ended above the trader; the veto twin against `TREND` (M3's method, `VETO_HELPS`, `VETO_HURTS` or `UNCLEAR`, at 95%); each month's equity; C1 and C2; agreement with the live trader's own decisions on the hours both exist (2026-10-28 13:45 UTC to 2026-11-01, a fidelity reading only).

**The trial ledger:** one trial (the masked trader in the picked window) with four measures; a second stage (§4.1) is a second trial; the veto twin, the shadow and the probe carry no promotion. No result changes the app; a `PASS` is evidence for a later plan on the owner's word.

**Said before it runs** (the thread's expectation, an opinion): at this risk the daily returns' interval is roughly ±27% a year over 61 days and ±11% over 365 (`CALCULATED` from a 0.3% daily volatility), so M1 to M3 come out `UNCLEAR` unless the model is far better or worse than the rules, in two months almost surely; M4 has 1,464 paired steps in two months (8,760 in the year) and can tell about 0.05% an hour (0.02%), so it is the sharpest reading; the live record (57.8% right, the rule the same, [TRADING](../../TRADING.md#the-record-so-far)) suggests `NO_DIFFERENCE`; costs and funding pull the account below 10,000 if it trades as often as live.

### 4.10 The trading review (TRADING.md's eight questions)

1. **Rules:** paper only, simulated accounts, no ledger, no order, no fill outside the simulation; research on the owner's word (09:15:02 UTC). The model's answers move only the simulated account, through code.
2. **Class:** research, a backtest; the live trader sees and does nothing differently, so the 2026-11-16 reading does not restart (by design: no live template, prompt, context or definition changes). The install composes a new profile with generation 15's hashes and the agents' check runs again.
3. **Costs:** the live fees, slippage and funding, as 0051.
4. **Horizon:** the trader is asked at its live horizon (a 1-hour call, holds of 4 to 24 hours), hourly instead of every 15 minutes.
5. **The model's place:** forecasting from numbers, with no text (no news): the case TRADING expects least from (principle 5); the veto without headlines likewise.
